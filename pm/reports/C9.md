## Summary

**1. Sound in the game.** `src/app/attach-audio.ts` attaches C7's runtime to the App: one line in `main.ts`
(`const audio = await attachAudio(app, world)`), between the renderer and the UI, as the brief's pattern asks.
- **Feeds.** Every packet of the shown world goes to `onRenderPacket`: its EVENTS through sounds.yaml's event map, and
  its AUDIO_WORKING into working loops. Historical events, and events older than 30 ticks, stay silent (§15.6), and a
  new state generation resets the SFX. The HUD's records (the UI's own HUD subscription) give the music feed. The
  camera gives the listener after every packet and frame. The host's `clock` event gives the world's pause, for any
  reason (the game menu, the player's pause, a hidden page); test mode is excepted, because its worlds are paused by
  design and stepped.
- **Sounds the page plays itself.** The local character's mover, as the renderer draws it, gives two kinds:
  - a footstep on each run cycle (2.3 tiles; about 3.9 a second at running speed, A4's "about 3.7");
  - the hammer's strike once per swing of the mining clip (at 0.6 of each second, where C6's clip strikes).

  Neither plays when the world skips more than 30 ticks between packets (a catch-up, a load), and at most two of
  each play per packet.
- **Unlock.** The page's first gesture (pointerdown or keydown, in the capture phase, so inside the handler) creates
  and resumes the AudioContext; any later gesture retries while the browser keeps it blocked (07 §18, §9.4).
- **The UI drives it through a structural `UiAudio`** (`src/ui/host.ts`):
  - GUI clicks: every button's click plays `ui-click` (Factorio's gui_click), and so does E on a green button;
  - the volumes: **Settings → Sound** has I §4.20's eight categories as sliders, and clicking a label mutes it, as in
    Factorio 2.0.36+. The sliders are heard at once, Back restores, and Confirm keeps them in `paperwork.settings`;
  - the scene: the main menu plays the charter piece's recording, and a game plays its program, seeded by the map
    seed (a load: the save name, hashed).
- **Crafting** sounds through `item_crafted`, which now maps to `ui-click` in sounds.yaml's event map. M1c's
  `craft-finished` replaces it; no new sound id, so prototypes.bin and the golden set do not change.
- **Verified in Chrome** (`e2e/game-audio.spec.ts`, @smoke):
  - the menu plays the charter recording;
  - menu clicks, footsteps, mining strikes, crafting and building sound, each by name;
  - the Sound tab reaches the mixer;
  - the game menu's pause closes the world gate in real time.

  The recorded session, rendered offline over Era I through the real engine, measures −16.6 LUFS with a −4.5 dBFS
  peak, no non-finite or denormal samples, and 7 SFX played.
- **`npm run assets` now ends with the audio build** (C7's open issue 5), so one command makes a playable build.

**3. The ore patch.** The grid came from the **art**, in two faults.
- **C6's pile** gathered its big chunks at each tile's centre, so a field of tiles drew a lattice of heaps.
- **Its depletion bands ran the wrong way.** The art contract said "band 7 is the full pile", while the sim and the
  golden model's rule 24 make band 0 the richest tile (Factorio's stage order). So a tile being mined would have
  grown.

The renderer's placement was right: it picks the shape by the position hash and the band from the packet. The fixes:
- **The pile is now A3's**: 11–13 chunks spread over the tile and a little past it, removed in a size-weighted random
  order, with band 0 the full pile.
- **The renderer draws the deposit's ground** under resource tiles: dirt, as the approved mocks put ore on dirt. It
  comes from the tile-id texture's high bit, and is blended like any soft tile edge.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The ore's band order (art).** The resource contract (`lib/contract.py`) now says band 0 is the full pile, as
   10 §15.4's `band`, rule 24 and Factorio's `stage_counts` order it. The fault was C6's; the sim and the golden set
   are unchanged.
2. **The deposit's ground (renderer)** deviates from 10 §6.5 and §9.9, which name no stain under resources.
   - The approved mocks put every resource tile on the dirt tile (`design/art/proto/layouts.py`), and the brief
     asks for the mocks' look.
   - It is a presentation flag in the tile-id texture: no memory, and no new texture. The shader's fast path covers
     the uniform case, so it costs 16 more fetches only on boundary pixels.
   - `TERRAIN.depositTile` is `dirt`. The tile set has fewer than 128 ids, or the stain is off.
3. **Mining's sound comes from the mover's `mining` flag**, a strike per swing of the clip. The sim emits no
   `entity_mined` for a resource unit (rule 23), so sounds.yaml's `byKind.resource` never fires for hand mining.
   Mining a chest (24 ticks) seldom reaches a swing's strike, and its `entity_mined` plays `deconstruct`. M1c's
   per-material map (tree, rock) needs the target's kind.
4. **Crafting** maps `item_crafted` to `ui-click` until M1c's `craft-finished`. Adding a sound would renumber the
   sound ids, which would change prototypes.bin and the golden set's prototypes (a value bump).
5. **Footsteps: one per run cycle** of the sprite's clip (`cycleTiles` 2.3), from the distance the mover covers.
   That is about 3.9 a second at running speed, close to A4's "about 3.7", and follows the stride rather than a
   clock.
6. **Settings → Sound**:
   - the eight categories of I §4.20 and C7's mixer, 0–100 %;
   - a label click mutes, as in Factorio 2.0.36+ (its changelog: "mute sound category by clicking its label");
   - labels: "Master volume", "Music volume", "Game effects volume" and "GUI effects volume" are provenance
     `factorio 23`; "Alerts volume", "Environment sounds volume", "Walking sound volume" and "Ambient sounds volume"
     are `proposal`s for the text owner, because I could not confirm Factorio 2.0's exact wording.
7. **The music seed of a loaded game** is the save's name, hashed (FNV-1a). The UI does not know a loaded world's
   map seed. A game keeps its music across loads.
8. **SFX that are still decoding** (the first gesture's clicks) are dropped quietly. The warning "has no decoded
   files" now appears only after the load has finished, when it means a fault.
9. **Benchmarks:**
   - the walk's full plan is exactly 10 minutes: a 6,600-tick warm-up, then 7 × 4,200 ticks (36,000), natively and
     in WebAssembly; the old plan was 35,400 natively and 31,200 in WebAssembly;
   - per-phase times are native only: a module built without simcheck has no clock, by D13 and D15;
   - the native-only gate metrics are listed (`actions_per_s`, `max_regenerations_per_tick`), and any other missing
     metric fails.
10. **The dev endpoints' limits:**
    - 256 MiB a file, 32 files and 512 MiB a report;
    - 500 reports and 4 GiB in all (507 past them: the user's evidence is never deleted here);
    - 4 uploads at once (503 with Retry-After).

    Writes need a loopback Host and a same-origin Origin and Sec-Fetch-Site. A request without them, such as
    `curl`'s, is a local program's, and is accepted.
11. **Playwright: 4 workers, fully parallel.** Every test has its own browser context. A spec whose tests depend on
    each other declares serial mode (trace, game-audio, bench). The dev server no longer watches `bench/`. A report
    written there by one worker's test might otherwise have reloaded another worker's page: one Firefox audio test
    lost its page once under parallel load, and the cause was not confirmed.
12. **Ports**: 21000–29999, below the kernel's ephemeral range (32768–60999), each claimed by a lock file in
    `~/.cache/paperwork/ports`. That is a user-wide directory, because TMPDIR differs between shells.
13. **The CI's skip is all-or-nothing on the whole input key**, plus the Python spec lane on its own key.
    Finer-grained skips would need each lane's exact inputs, which are hard to enumerate safely.
14. **Files outside an obvious scope**, each small:
    - C7's `system.ts`: the `recording` and `sfxReady` status fields, and `onPlayed`;
    - C7's `sfx.ts` and `sounds.ts` (the decode drop) and `offline.ts` (`script` renders);
    - `src/dev/audio.ts` (the render reports the script's SFX);
    - C4's terrain files, C6's art scripts;
    - `vite.config.ts` (the watcher), the Playwright config;
    - C8's harness files named by the audit.

## Proposed implementation decisions (for pm/DECISIONS-IMPL.md)

- **The merge gate is `npm run ci`**, green before a work package reports done. `npm run ci:fast` is for iterating.
  A green gate records its inputs' key and skips itself when nothing it reads changed.
- **Resource bands are Factorio's stage order everywhere**: band 0 is the richest tile, in the sim (rule 24), the
  art's `pieces(band, n)` and the renderer.
- **A deposit lies on its own ground** (`TERRAIN.depositTile`, dirt), as the approved mocks draw it. It is a
  presentation flag of the terrain pass, never sim state.
- **Sounds the page plays itself** (footsteps, mining strikes, GUI clicks) come from what is drawn: the mover's run
  cycle and mining clip, and the UI's buttons. They are never sounds of historical motion: a gap of more than 30
  ticks between packets plays nothing.

## M0 exit audit response (`pm/reviews/M0-exit.md`)

2. **Fixed: the canonical comparison window restarts after late reloads and joins** (8596513).
   - `record_windows` keeps every tick of the 2,000 after each reload, in the reference and the variant alike, and
     `check_equivalence` keeps the union of the variants' windows.
   - Every replacement is compared at once: the fresh module's canonical state must equal the state it was saved
     from, and the state kept for that tick is the fresh module's.
   - `compare` refuses traces whose kept ticks differ, so a missing window cannot be skipped silently.
   - The multi-client Room compares full canonical states after every tick for 2,000 ticks after each join, crash
     and re-sync.
   - Tests: `harness::a_late_reload_restarts_the_dense_window` (a divergence 4 ticks after a late reload is found at
