# D1: M1 data readiness (typed design data and the engine overlay for the slice)
Status: done

## Summary

The design data and the engine overlay now hold, typed and checked, every number M1's systems read. The M1
packages can build against them without inventing a value.

**Typed design data** (`design/spec/data`; validator, SCHEMA.md, data-stage schemas):
- **Entities:**
  - pole and Switch supply areas (`supply_area_distance`, Factorio's own field) replace the prose "5x5" and
    "9x9", and the Switch's profile is an enum (`inverse-sqrt`);
  - undergrounds and splitters carry `items_per_s`;
  - steam generators carry their steam flow and temperature window;
  - the radar carries its two scans;
  - the Frontier cluster carries its payloads;
  - the artillery turret's `auto_targets` is an enum.
- **Fixes to "ISO" numbers that were not Factorio 2.0's:**
  - electric inserters' `kw` (1.1's display values; 2.0 shows rotation + extension power);
  - the day length (25,000 ticks was 1.1's; 2.0 is 25,200);
  - rock hand-mining times (2, 3 and 2 s, not 1.5, 2 and 1).
- **meta:**
  - `heat.daylight` (the day cycle);
  - the staffing and world cadences;
  - worldgen v1's numbers: `world.biomes` (01 §2), `world.patches` (01 §4, which `resources.autoplace` only
    pointed at in prose), `world.towns` (01 §5), the records stores (01 §6.2) and `start.guarantees` (01 §9).

**The engine overlay** (`game/data/engine`). Factorio 2.0.77's prototype values for all 32 slice entities and their
tier siblings:
- boxes, mining times, fast-replace groups and upgrades;
- inserter vectors and swing speeds;
- drill mining areas and drop points;
- the offshore pump's pumping position;
- fluid boxes; lamps; pole wiring; burner and furnace slots;
- collision masks (10 §4.4's layers) and chart map colours;
- the belt lane geometry; Factorio's item subgroups and orders;
- map colours of resources and world objects;
- terrain materials per tile (new `terrain.yaml`);
- M1c's planned sound map (`sounds.yaml` `planned`).

The data stage (`overlay.ts`, new `engine.ts`):
- resolves all of these per entity, with the trimmed player box and the mining area derived;
- checks every capability manifest's prototypes complete and consistent, strictly. For example, an electric
  inserter's `kw` must equal its swing's power, and every fluid connection must face out of its footprint.

**The M1 capability manifest** (`capabilities.yaml` `m1`):
- It extends m0 and takes 09 §2.7's slice filter.
- It names M1's actions, projections, queries, packet sections, registry entries, rule scenarios, benchmarks and
  systems.
- It names the META blocks (`data`) and the other design-data parts (`reads`, new) its engine reads.
- Every manifest is now checked, current or not. `current` stays m0.
- With `current: m1`, the data stage prints exactly what the M1 packages still have to emit
  (see Verification).

How to use it:
- `node scripts/data-stage.ts --manifest m1` builds the slice strictly, with no `--allow-incomplete`.
- `runDataStage(...).model` exposes each entity's resolved `engine` fields to tests and tools.
- `gamedev-python tools/validate_spec.py design/spec/data` runs the new `check_engine_values`.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **No schema change** (brief: "unless a typed field must reach the engine"). Nothing new enters
   `prototypes.bin` or `prototypes.json`: no M1 engine code reads these fields yet, and each M1 package will
   shape its own columns. The overlay is typed and checked, and `runDataStage().model` exposes it.
   - The m1 manifest's `data` and `reads` are the exact list to emit.
   - The contents record is unchanged, so M0's `prototypes.bin` is byte-identical. This is why `heat.daylight`
     sits under `meta.heat` (beside `sunlight`; a new top-level block would add an omitted part) and why the
     worldgen tables sit under `meta.world`. It is also why `heat.into` was not typed (row M3 below).
2. **Worldgen blocks cite their section in `note`, not `formula_ref`.** The formula_refs registry (a schema file)
   lacks 01 §2, §4 and §5. The worldgen package adds them with its META records and its `// @formula` tags.
3. **`supply_area_distance`, not a side length.** Factorio's field and Lua API semantics are half the square from
   the pole's centre. The UI shows twice it, as Factorio does.
4. **Electric inserters' `kw`** is defined (SCHEMA.md) as Factorio 2.0's maximum movement power, without drain,
   following the design's convention for machines (`kw` plus `drain_kw`). The data stage checks it against the
   overlay's swing speeds.
5. **The offshore pump keeps the design's 1 × 2 size.** 2.0's pump is 1 × 1 and pumps from the tile it faces;
   its numbers are translated into the 1 × 2 frame (mirrors.yaml says how).
6. **Collision masks map 2.0's layers onto 10 §4.4's seven.** Factorio's is_object, is_lower_object, floor,
   transport_belt and meltable layers only keep belts, pipes to ground and heat pipes walkable while they still
   collide with buildings. The mapped masks keep that behaviour.
7. **The trimmed player box is derived** (10 §4.4: "the data stage derives the trimmed boxes"). Chests, walls and
   gates are exempt.
8. **M1c's sounds are a `planned` block**, because `build.py` refuses a declared sound without a generator.
   The block is an addition to the audio package's file.
9. **New capability-manifest keys:**
   - `systems` and `reads`, plus `prototypes: {filter: …}`. schema-gen ignores them, since it reads only the
     current manifest's names;
   - every manifest is checked, and m1's engine overlay is checked strictly in every data-stage run, so it
     cannot regress before M1 starts.
10. **Overlay keys must name something.** A key in prototypes.yaml, mirrors.yaml, terrain.yaml or sounds.yaml's
    maps that names no prototype, no used mirrors id or no tile is an error. A typo can no longer hide an entry.
11. **Readers of the changed fields** were edited:
    - `tools/render_spec.py` (EXTRA_KEYS, and the patch numbers in 02 §1) — not listed in the brief, but it reads
      the renamed fields;
    - `DataStageResult.model` was added;
    - checkEngine's sound-coverage check runs on the engine's own `sounds.yaml` only. The fake sim's override
      maps only its scenes; the existing override test caught this.
12. **Factual corrections to other chapters:**
    - 01 §3 and 10 §5.7 (the day length; R3's P7 had asked for this audit);
    - 01 §2.2 (the rock times).
