# C6: Asset pipeline v0 (Survey)
Status: done

## Summary

`cd game && npm run assets` builds every M0 asset headlessly from committed sources, in the *Survey* look, into
`public/` (gitignored): KTX2 texture-array pages, the sprite manifest v2, terrain materials, the UI skin, the DOM
icon sheets and the font subsets. It caches by content hash in `.cache/assets/`, checks what it built against 10 §9.5
and writes review material to `.cache/assets/qc/`. CLAUDE.md has an assets section with the commands.

**The pipeline** (`game/scripts/build-assets.ts` drives `game/scripts/assets/*.py` and Blender):
1. `plan.py` lists 128 sets: the model scripts, a greybox per entity of `entities.yaml`, item icons, `fx/`.
2. **Render** (Blender 5.2.2 Cycles, batches of 40 sets, `taskset -c 8-15,24-31`, 16 threads). The rig, camera and
   passes are A3's, ported to `game/assets/blender/lib/` with the model contract of 10 §9.1 (`META`, `build`,
   `pose`; documented in `lib/contract.py`). Render only what moves, by B2's scheme: F₀ in full, the layer
   rectangles R (merged when they intersect), each layer frame rendered inside R with a border render, one
   Normal-resolution shadow, a verification render of the middle frame. Inserters render platform, arm and hands
   as parts with pivots. The character renders 288 frames (8 orientations × idle 4, run 16, mine 16) with their
   shadows.
3. **Post** (4 workers): the Survey ink (silhouette, inner and crease lines from 2× normal and aux passes, hatching
   of the dark band, edge darkening, mottle, grain) with canvas-coordinate fields, glow, trim on 8 px, base pieces
   (F₀ minus each R), shadows, and the position-pass cut of the belts (below).
4. **Groups** (§9.7): `core`, then each tier's unlocking technologies merged up to 3 MB.
5. **Pack** (maxrects-packer) into block-aligned cells: colour 32-px cells with 8-px gutters, shadows and icons
   16/4, light 8/2, so no 4×4 block of any level mixes two cells.
6. **Assemble and encode**: our own box-filtered levels of the premultiplied page go to basisu 2.50 as a mipmapped
   DDS (basisu keeps supplied levels); XUBC7 quality 90 for colour, light and icons, ETC1S `-q 255` for shadows;
   colour split into `.h.ktx2` (level 0) and `.n.ktx2` (levels 1–3). Files are named by content hash.
7. **Terrain** materials for worldgen v0's six tiles (512², 8×8 tiles, XUBC7, 3 levels), the **UI skin** (33 SVG
   parts, the paper fibre, 21 glyphs), **fonts** (7 WOFF2 subsets, 152 kB, OFL; the text canon fully covered), the
   **DOM icon sheets** (WebP lossless, 32 and 64 px, per group).
8. `public/atlases/sprites.json` (manifest v2, explicit index tables, schema `sprites.schema.json`) and
   `inventory.json` (the byte gate of §9.7).
9. **Checks** (`checks.py`, `quality.py`): schema and manifest validation, coverage (every prototype has a set or
   greybox, every icon a set), cells, the composite check, registration, the 3 % rule, shadow direction, the
   fixture cases, compression, fallback formats and a per-page cell-isolation probe through every format.
10. **QC** (`--no-qc` skips it): contact sheets and GIFs (`contact.py`), A3's legibility metrics (`metrics.py`),
    the M0 scene drawn from the *encoded* pages as the renderer will draw it (`preview.py` with `emulate.py`; zoom
    1 and 2, dpr 2, night), the projection calibration, the UI skin screenshotted in Chrome at dpr 1 and 2, and
    the budgets (`budget.py`).

**Art.** M0's set at A3's quality: the character (idle, run, mine; 8 orientations; per-frame shadows), the iron
chest, the iron-ore patch (6 shapes × 8 depletion bands), 15 item icons from A3's item models plus `fx/missing`,
smoke puffs, terrain (grass, dirt, sand, water and deep water with water-lining, out-of-map), the UI skin and the
fonts. Beyond M0, to exercise every drawing program before M1: the keypunch (two animated layers: flywheel and
crank; cards, punch and 27 keys; working and idle; light; smoke), the transport belt (20 pieces × 16 frames,
connection masks) and the inserter and burner inserter. Greyboxes for all 104 entities.

**Fixture.** `game/test/fixtures/sprites/` (10 §15.7's path): one set of every kind (stone furnace with idle and a
layer, belt with masks, inserter with pivots, character with its three clips, rail signal with lights, tree with
shape × stage, resource with shape × band, locomotive with 64 orientations, radar with an animated shadow, icons),
1,162 frames on uncompressed `rgba8` KTX2 pages (573 kB) so the renderer needs no transcoder to test, and
`cases.json`: 64 packet-shaped inputs with the expected draws, computed by `manifest.py`, the Python twin of the
renderer's lookup. `npm run assets -- --check` runs them without Blender.

**Numbers** (this host): cold build 12 min (Blender 601 s, of which the character 389 s; post 44 s), warm 30 s.
Download: `core` 2.47 MB at Normal (High level 0 +2.89 MB); the first frame of a new game 4.07 MB against the 5 MB
gate. GPU: 42.0 MB at High (BC7), 13.5 MB at Normal, 28.3 MB at Low (RGBA8).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The compression gate is calibrated; 10 §9.5 #5's numbers are warnings.** The spec asks PSNR ≥ 40 dB at High
   and ≥ 37 at Normal, dE p99 ≤ 6 and detail ≥ 0.98. Pages of large sprites pass (core pages 0 and 1: 46.6–47.5 dB
   at High, 41.8–44.0 at Normal). Edge-dense pages do not: the character, belt, ore and inserter pages measure
   40.0–42.8 dB at High but 34.0–35.9 dB with dE p99 8.1–10.9 at Normal; icons 36.8–40.5 dB at High and 33.0–36.0 at
   Normal.
   - This is BC7 itself: quality 90, 100 and lossless XUBC7 differ by < 0.2 dB; the slower bc7e base encoder gains
     0.8–1.3 dB for 13–22 % more bytes.
   - The spec's numbers were measured on B2's page of large machine sprites. Through our level scheme the same page
     gives dE p99 6.28 at Normal and 36.5 dB at level 2, and §9.2's own table gives icons dE p99 8.8.
   - Source and decoded pages side by side at 3× show no visible difference.
   - The gate (`checks.py` `GATE`): colour and light ≥ 38 dB / dE p99 ≤ 8 at High and ≥ 32 / ≤ 13 at Normal; icons
     ≥ 34 / ≤ 13 and ≥ 30 / ≤ 14; detail ≥ 0.98: 2–3 dB and 2–5 dE under the worst M0 page of each kind. Levels below
     Normal are reported only, since the spec names High and Normal. Failures name the worst sprite.
2. **Shadows** (§9.1, §9.2).
   - **The shadow pass makes the model a pure occluder**: invisible to camera, diffuse, glossy, transmission and
     volume rays. The Survey washes are emission, so the model lit its own shadow, noisily (interior noise 3–5×
     B2's masks; 27 dB after ETC1S). Masks are now clean, so greyboxes use 16 samples.
   - Clean masks have hard edges. ETC1S codes small hard shapes at 28–37 dB mask PSNR (B2's soft masks: 36 dB).
     §9.2 calls the shadow "a soft mask that the shader hatches", so the gate is the hatched paper as the player sees
     it at zoom 1: dE p99 ≤ 6 (measured ≤ 4.1, mean ≤ 0.23), plus a 26 dB floor on the mask.
3. **Greyboxes: one image per footprint orientation** (§9.2 "Placeholders"). Square footprints get one image and
   non-square rotatable ones two (north–south, east–west, as the steam engine's two-way map). Four directions with a
   front stripe made greyboxes 68 % of core's colour cells and core 4.0 MB, over the gate. Core is now 2.47 MB.
4. **Fallback formats** (§9.5 #5 "held to §9.2's measured quality ±10 %") are judged by their ratio to BC7 on the
   same page, since the absolute numbers follow the content. Beyond §9.2's ratio +10 % warns, beyond 1.5× fails.
   ASTC 4×4 measures 1.33–1.44× BC7's dE mean here against §9.2's 1.14–1.22×.
5. **Cells per array** (§9.2, decision 50: 32-px cells, 8-px gutters). Colour keeps 32/8. Shadows and icons (3
   levels) use 16/4, and light (2 levels) 8/2: each is block-aligned down to its own lowest level. The cell probe
   checks this: every page's layout in solid colours, encoded and transcoded to BC7, ASTC 4×4, BC3, ETC2, BC4 and EAC
   R11. The worst texel is 0.020 off its own cell's colour (ETC2), against B2's 0.016. Every frame's bilinear
   footprint at every level stays in its cell.
6. **The UI skin is one hashed CSS of SVG data URIs** (§9.3 says resvg renders 1× and 2× into one WebP sheet for
   `border-image`). CSS `border-image` cannot address a sub-rectangle of a sheet, so a raster sheet would need one
   file per part. The CSS (21.6 kB) is one request and crisp at every dpr. resvg still renders a 1×/2× sheet for QC.
   Glyphs are a `<symbol>` sprite with the titles stripped: the UI labels them from the text canon.
7. **Manifest additions** (§15.7; all in `sprites.schema.json`):
   - top level: `style` (`survey-1`); `terrain` (per tile: file, bytes, SHA-256, group; size, levels, `pxPerTile`,
     codec); `dom` (`icons` and `iconSheets` per group and size, `ui` {css, fibre, glyphs}, `fonts`);
   - set fields: `smoke` (chimney points, level-0 px from the anchor), `framesPerTile` (belts, §6.8's formula),
     `shadowShoulder` (inserters), `lightFrom: "status"` (signals; in the fixture), `radiusTiles` (`fx/smoke`);
   - inserter shadows are parts `<part>_shadow` with their own pivots: the ground footprint, turned with the part
     about the shadow's shoulder point;
   - belts are full-frame sets: `base` [dir, mask, frame], `shadow` [dir, mask], no `layers`.
8. **The belt connection mask is the sim's to define** (§15.7's `mask` axis maps 256 values through `map.mask`). The
   build reads it as bit 0 fed from behind, bit 1 from the left, bit 2 from the right, bit 3 output connects, and
   draws a curve, a straight or the caps as Factorio does (`transport_belt.py`). This is a proposal for the sim
   package. Belt pieces are modelled half a tile past their open edges and cut at the tile edge (+0.03 tile) by a
   16-bit position pass, so runs join without ink seams; shadows come from the exact-length piece.
9. **Depletion bands** (§15.7 `band`): band 7 is the full pile, and band b keeps max(2, round(n (0.16 + 0.84 b/7)))
   of the shape's chunks, removed smallest first.
10. **Art decisions against the metrics** (A3 §3.2, §7; 10 §9.5 #4):
    - **the Report icon** is pale green card stock (L\* 90) with the green as its binding tape. items.yaml's "green
      cover" is kept, and DIRECTION §3.2's "information is paper" now holds: A3's all-green cover measured L\* 56,
      and the same-hue pair with Logic board was 3.8 L\* apart;
    - **the inserter base plate** is mid grey and the boom 0.20 m wide (13 px at High), so the arm's tier colour
      survives its outline at 1×. Inserter figure-ground is −0.59 (A3: −0.25…−0.60);
    - **the iron chest** is a knee-high strongbox (0.56 m; in this projection height reads 1:1, so a 1 m box read as
      a locker);
    - **the keypunch** shows its firebox burning by day (A3 §8: lit emitters show work); the flywheel's pedestal
      stands on the stove.
11. **Render PNGs carry no metadata**: the rig switches off Blender's stamps and strips the render times Cycles
    writes into each file, so identical pixels give identical bytes (Verification: determinism).
12. **Pages hold premultiplied, display-encoded values**, as §9.1's verified composite: the renderer must upload BC7
    as `COMPRESSED_RGBA_BPTC_UNORM_EXT`, never the sRGB variant, and blend premultiplied.
13. **Writes outside the brief's list**, each minimal:
    - `game/test/fixtures/sprites/` (the path 10 §15.7 names for the tiny atlas) and `game/test/assets/` (the
      calibration test);
    - `game/biome.json`: three ignore lines for the build's outputs (`public/atlases`, `public/sheets`,
      `public/fonts`), like C1a's `public/data`;
    - `tools/check-toolchain.sh`: basisu 2.50 (already installed by B2), jsonschema and an XUBC7 round trip, as
      the brief asks;
    - `jsonschema` installed into `gamedev-python` with `uv pip`.
    The source SVGs got `<title>`s for Biome's a11y rule; the outputs strip them.

     +14 MB for the RGBA8 Normal fallback (≈ 166/180 against ≤ 192).

## Review response (codex C6-r1)

Twelve findings: all twelve fixed; finding 9 rests on one reading of decision 13 that the manager should confirm
(below). The C6 agent was gone, so a second agent did this round in its own worktree. Verification is at the end.

1. **Critical: KTX2 metadata contradicts the pixels. Fixed.**
   - Every KTX2 file now states the upload contract in its data format descriptor (`game/scripts/assets/ktx2.py`).
     The values are premultiplied and display-encoded, and the renderer samples them as UNORM. So the transfer
     function is `KHR_DF_TRANSFER_LINEAR`, and `KHR_DF_FLAG_ALPHA_PREMULTIPLIED` is set on the colour, light,
     icon and terrain arrays (shadow masks: 0).
   - XUBC7 is encoded with basisu's `-linear` (the new `encode.py`), so basisu itself writes the linear transfer
     function. This also switches the encoder's error metric from sRGB weights to linear ones. Measured on the
     M0 pages: within 0.1 dB and 0.02 ΔE of the sRGB setting, in BC7 and in every fallback format.
   - basisu has no option for the premultiplied flag, so the build sets it after encoding. The transcoder only
     reports the flag, and the pixel data is untouched.
   - `ktx2.contract_errors` checks every published file and every fixture file: vkFormat, supercompression
     scheme, colour model, primaries, transfer function, flags, size and level count. `encode.py` also refuses a
     non-conforming file before caching it.
     - On the C6 build's files the check reports 44 errors (transfer 2 and flags 0 on every colour, light, icon and
       terrain file). Now it reports 0.
   - The fixture's light page now carries the premultiplied flag as well (it was straight). Light is added, and
     its RGB never exceeds its alpha (alpha = max(RGB)). "Premultiplied" is therefore the declaration under which
     no loader multiplies again.
   - `tools/check-toolchain.sh`'s XUBC7 round trip now encodes with `-linear` and checks the linear transfer
     function.
2. **Major: the inserter shadow needs an undocumented renderer extension. Fixed: specified in §15.7's vocabulary.**
   - The output cannot avoid one shadow rule. A shadow cast from the shoulder's height is the ground footprint
     displaced by a fixed vector D (SHADOW_REACH × the shoulder's height). Rotating any image about the visible
     shoulder point Q turns D with θ, so at θ = ½ the shadow would fall north-west of the arm. The shadow must
     therefore turn about Q′ = Q + D.
   - The rule is now bound in §15.7's own terms (Q, Hp, k, S, θ, r, pivot, length) in three places:
     - `manifest.py`, the Python twin of the renderer's lookup. Its docstring ships in `cases.json` as `notes`.
     - `sprites.schema.json`, whose descriptions give the names and units.
     - The fixture's cases, which pin every shadow draw (`at`, `rotation_turns`, `stretch`, `scale`).
   - The rule:
     - With k′ = S ÷ the shadow array's `pxPerTile`, the shadow's shoulder point is Q′ = the anchor +
       `shadowShoulder` · k′. `shadowShoulder` is a set field in the shadow array's level-0 px: where the shoulder's
       shadow falls.
     - The hand's shadow point is Hp′ = Q′ + r · S · (sin 2πθ, −cos 2πθ).
     - `arm_shadow` is rotated by θ about its pivot placed at Q′, and stretched by the arm's factor
       |Hp − Q| ÷ (`length` · k).
     - The hand's shadow is rotated by θ about its pivot placed at Hp′.
     - `platform_shadow` [dir] is placed like any frame.
   - Every inserter frame's anchor is now its part's ground point: pivot − anchor equals `shoulder` for the arm
     and hands, and `shadowShoulder` for their shadows. `manifest.validate` checks it.
     - So a renderer can also place each part frame by the generic frame formula at the part's ground point (the
       anchor for the arm, the anchor + r · S · (sin, −cos) for a hand) and rotate it about its pivot. Both
       readings give one picture.
   - The spec text to add under §15.7's `inserter` program is in "Proposed spec text" below. Spec changes are the
     manager's to route.
3. **Major: the renderer fixtures violate B2's exact composition. Fixed.**
   - `fixture.py` builds animated sets from complete frames, exactly as the build does:
     - F₀ is packed whole as one cell, and the base pieces are sub-rectangles of that cell (F₀ minus R).
     - Each layer frame is a cell of R plus a gutter of that frame's own pixels.
     - The moving content stays 2 px inside R, so F₀ and every frame agree on R's band.
     - Cells sit on 2 px with a gutter of one texel at the array's lowest level (colour and icons 2 px, two
       levels; shadows and light 1 px, one level). So the level-1 box filter and bilinear reads stay in the cell.
   - The radar is now three base pieces around the dish's R; before, its whole base was drawn under the layer.
   - The furnace has a 22 %-opaque glass band crossing R's left and right edges: B2's translucent case.
   - The decoded composition is checked. The new `composite.py` draws the base pieces plus every layer from the
     decoded pages, premultiplied and bilinear, at each level, three scales and sub-pixel offsets. It compares
     the result with the whole frame drawn as one quad (F₀'s cell with each layer's rectangle replaced, its levels
     box-filtered on the page grid).
     - `checks.py --fixtures-only` (`npm run assets -- --check`, CI) runs it on the fixture.
     - On the committed fixture it reports ΔE 31.3 (furnace) and 24.3 (radar). On the regenerated one it reports
       0.00 for both.
     - The new fixture checks also catch every other old defect: the radar's undeclared `animShadow`, the
       inserter anchors and the light DFD flag.
   - **What changed for C1c** (`test/fixtures/sprites/`). The file names and the format (manifest v2, `rgba8`
     KTX2) are unchanged.
     - `arrays[0]` (colour): `pageSize` 256 → **512**. With real gutters the colour frames no longer fit one
       256² page, and a second page would have added files. So `colour-core-0.h.ktx2` is now 512² (1,048,840 B)
       and `colour-core-0.n.ktx2` 256² (262,408 B).
     - The colour and icon cells have 2-px gutters, so every colour and icon frame's page position (`x`, `y`)
