# C8: M0 integration, test harness and exit QA
Status: done. Every M0 exit criterion of 10 §12 passes (the table below); `npm run ci` is green three times in a row
under M0's capability manifest. Two things only the user's machine can settle stay open: the host factor and the GPU
and audio numbers of `bench.html`.

## Summary

**The M0 build, end to end.** On `dev` the App now runs C3's shell, C4's renderer and C5's UI (merged by me at
76f740f, no conflicts) on C2's sim. Through the real input layer a player starts a game from the menu, walks on
generated terrain at 60 UPS, mines ore by hand, crafts the iron chest, places it, fills it, empties it, mines it back,
reads production statistics, saves and loads; a trap reloads the last autosave and leaves a bug bundle that replays
natively. Audio is the one package not attached to the game page (open issue 1).

**What C8 added** (10 §12's Integration row):

- **The cross-engine trace, D18 (d)** (`e2e/trace.spec.ts`, @smoke). One trace core,
  `src/dev/trace/core.ts`, runs in Node in-process and in a browser module worker (the trace page,
  `test/trace.html`):
  - the *module trace* replays a closure log through the shell's own module layer (`SimInstance`, `ReplaySource`,
    blobs, `HISTORICAL`) and records d_t and r_t after every tick and the full state hash on scheduled ticks;
  - the *shell replay* runs the log through the real `SimShell` in its replay mode, which checks every d_t against
    the natively recorded digests; its digest ring, final hash and r are compared too.

  Natively, `simtool replay --trace --full` gives the same columns. `ci` runs the golden set (3,600 ticks, the full
  hash after every tick) and a new 10,000-tick session, `test/logs/m0-cross-engine-10k` (four players on a new
  world with water: worldgen, hand mining on pristine chunks, a chest built seven times, filled by Shift+LMB and by
  fast transfer, emptied and mined back, joins, a leave and rejoin, a fenced walk, noise actions). `npm run test:slow` runs every session in `test/logs/`, on the
  dev and the release module.
- **The multi-client harness, D18 (f)** (`sim::testkit::multi::Room`, `crates/sim/tests/rules/d18f_multi_client.rs`):
  one closure stream feeds several worlds. Clients join by checkpoint mid-run, crash and come back from their own
  checkpoint with a catch-up, are re-synced from another's snapshot, run NO_SLEEP, and read their worlds three ways
  (every tick with subscriptions, never, at random with queries). The clients agree on d_t and r_t every tick and on
  the full canonical state after every fault; a planted divergence is found and named (`PLAYERS … .x`).
- **Fuzzed closures, D18 (g) in WebAssembly** (`test/harness/closure-fuzz.test.ts`): 300 fast-check mutations of the
  golden log's closures (bit flips, truncations, insertions, header fields at their limits, doubled and random
  closures). None traps; a refused closure changes neither the state nor the tick.
- **The scenario DSL's layouts and helpers** (10 §10.2): `Scenario::from_layout` on ASCII layouts
  (`test/layouts/*.txt`; `sim::testkit::layout` and its TypeScript twin `test/helpers/layout.ts`), and `place`,
  `insert`, `entity_at`, `stats().produced(name)`, `hash`, `digest`, `reload`.
- **Frozen goldens** (10 §10.3's workflow): `test/goldens/*.json`, `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1`, a Rust and a Vitest helper.
  M0's golden ratios, each against Factorio's number and frozen: **GM1** 120 iron chests hand-crafted a minute
  (201 crafts in exactly 6,030 ticks), **GM2** 30 ore hand-mined a minute, **GM3** running 0.15 tiles a tick
  (90 tiles in 600 ticks, 90·√½ per axis diagonally), **GM4** an iron chest mined in 24 ticks, **GM5** an iron
  chest holds 3,200 plates.
- **The capability manifest's explicit errors** (`crates/sim/tests/rules/capabilities.rs`): all 31 player action
  types outside M0 are refused as `unsupported` and change nothing; the 3 projection kinds outside it answer
  status 2, reason 1; the 5 query kinds finish with ok 0, reason 1.
- **Benchmarks** (10 §10.6):
  - The browser runner (`BENCH=1 npx playwright test e2e/bench.spec.ts`, Chrome and Firefox) shares one scenario
    core with the Node runner (`src/dev/bench/sim-core.ts`). Its gates and history lines match simtool's.
  - **`bench.html`**, for the user's laptop (`http://127.0.0.1:5173/bench.html`). Its sections:
    - the simulation's M0 scenarios in a worker;
    - a determinism check: the golden log and the 10,000-tick session against their natively recorded digests
      (in whatever browser opens the page: Safari too, which this host cannot run);
    - render on C4's §6.12 scenes: the main thread's time, the GPU's (timer query, else a readPixels drain) and
      the frame rate;
    - §9.4's audio gate, as offline renders;
    - the GPU's limits, extensions and §9.2's format probe.

    It POSTs everything to `/__bench`. The page and its workers are cross-origin isolated, which gives 5 µs timers
    in Chrome and 20 µs in Firefox instead of 0.1 ms and 1 ms.
  - The calibration: `node bench/calibrate.ts` turns the user's results into the measured host factor.
  - The A/B in one session: bench.html's `?ab=1` mode, which `npm run bench:ab -- --base <rev> --runner chrome`
    drives with both trees' modules, interleaved in alternating order.
- **The dev server's endpoints** (10 §3.5; `scripts/dev-endpoints-plugin.ts`):
  - `POST /__bench`;
  - `POST /__report/<id>/<file>`: Ctrl+Alt+F12 or `__game.report(note)` sends the bundle. That is the base save, the
    closure log with its digests, the digest ring, the console (the page's and the workers'), a screenshot of the
    world from the GPU, and `client.json`; after a trap, the trap's own bundle. It lands in `bench/reports/<id>/`, and
    `simtool replay bench/reports/<id>` replays it;
  - `POST /__shot` (`__game.shot(name)`).
- **Playwright picks free ports** (`scripts/free-port.ts`), so parallel worktrees no longer collide; `PW_PORT` still
  pins one.
- **M0's exit e2e** (`e2e/m0-exit.spec.ts`, @smoke, Chrome and Firefox with `?render=none`), the Node chest test
  (`test/harness/chest-shell.test.ts`), the endpoints' test (`scripts/dev-endpoints.test.ts`), and the smoke
  screenshots (`e2e/screenshots.spec.ts`, @shots → `pm/reports/C8/`).
- `npm run test:slow` and `npm run bench:ab`; `ci` builds simtool in its cargo lane; CLAUDE.md has a *Testing and
  QA* section.

### How to play the M0 build

1. On the host: `cd ~/browsergame/game && npm run dev`. The dev server listens on `127.0.0.1:5173`. The art must be
   built once (`npm run assets`); it is built on this host now.
2. On your laptop: `ssh -L 5173:127.0.0.1:5173 cpuserver10`, then open **http://127.0.0.1:5173/**.
3. **New game → Play**. The seed defaults to 1945; its starting iron patch is a few tiles north-east of the spawn
   point.

**Controls** (Factorio's defaults; *Settings → Controls* rebinds any of them):

| Key or button | Does |
|---|---|
| W A S D | walk |
| RMB, held | mine: a resource tile, or a chest |
| E | the character screen; LMB on a recipe crafts 1, RMB 5, Shift+LMB all |
| LMB on an inventory stack | take it into the hand; LMB on the ground then builds it |
| Q | put the hand away, or pick the item under the cursor (pipette) |
| LMB on a chest | open it. Shift+LMB on a stack moves it across, Ctrl+LMB moves every stack of that item |
| Ctrl+LMB on a chest in the world | put the hand's stack in (fast transfer) |
| P | production statistics |
| Esc | the game menu: Save game, Load game, Settings, Exit to main menu |
| Mouse wheel; F9 / Shift+F9 | zoom (0.3–3×); zoom 1× / 2× |
| Alt | alt-mode |

**What M0 contains:** you start with 8 iron plates, enough for one iron chest (the only recipe). There is iron ore to
mine by hand, but nothing smelts it yet. There is no day cycle (§5.7 is M1's), no sound in the game page (open issue
1), and no trees or decoratives (worldgen v0).

**Helping the QA:**
- Ctrl+Alt+F12 (or `__game.report('what happened')` in the console) sends a bug report to `bench/reports/`, which
  replays natively. Some Linux desktops take Ctrl+Alt+F12 for a console switch; the console call always works.
- `__game.shot('name')` sends a screenshot of the world.
- **http://127.0.0.1:5173/bench.html → Run everything** (about two minutes, tab in front) measures your machine and
  sends the results back. This is the one thing the host cannot measure for itself: the host factor, your GPU's
  frame times and formats, and the audio gate.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The trace page runs two paths** (10 §10.5 says "loads a closure log and its start save in the real sim worker,
   replays it with extraction off, and posts the per-tick digest trace and final hash").
   - The real `SimShell` replays the log in a worker. It checks every d_t against the natively recorded ones and
     yields its digest ring and final state.
   - The shell's module layer (`SimInstance`, `ReplaySource`) gives the per-tick r_t and the full state hash,
     which `SimShell` does not expose.

   Both run the same code in Node and in the browser. No shell code changed.
2. **The trace core lives in `src/dev/trace/core.ts`**, not `test/`, so that bench.html's determinism section
   reuses it (check-arch forbids `src/` importing `test/`). The trace page itself is `test/trace.html`, as specified.
3. **The full state hash schedule.** Every tick for a log of at most 3,600 ticks, then D18 a's cadence for longer
   logs (every tick to 2,000, every 600, the last). §10.4 #6 says "≤ 2,000 ticks: the golden set", but the golden log
   is 3,600 ticks long, so every tick of it is hashed. The module trace sets `HISTORICAL` as the shell's replay does,
   while `simtool` does not, so the comparison also shows the flag changes no state.
4. **The 10,000-tick log** is a new scenario, `crates/sim/tests/rules/cross_engine.rs`. M0 has no scripted opening
   (09 §7.1 is M1's), so it scripts one plus three other players.
5. **The DSL extends C2's `sim::testkit`** (`layout.rs`, `multi.rs`, helpers in `scenario.rs`), as the brief's
   "reusing what exists" asks; C2 is done, so nothing collided.
   - A layout is the start state, created as a fixture and loaded through `sim_load`, as the benchmark factories
     do. Everything after it goes through closures, `place` and `insert` included (§10.2).
   - The format adds two things to §10.2's legend: `@`, the spawn tile, which places the grid; and `:items=`,
     container contents.
   - §10.2's infinite power, source and sink test entities need M1's prototypes.
6. **Goldens:** one JSON file per test (`test/goldens/<file>.json`), so parallel tests never write the same file.
   `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` stands for §10.3's `--update-goldens`. M0's golden ratios GM1–GM5 are additions: §10.3's
   G1–G20 all need M1+ systems.
7. **The dev endpoints' server half is `scripts/dev-endpoints-plugin.ts`**, beside the other Vite plugins, because it
   is Node code (tsconfig.scripts) and `src/dev/` is browser code. The client half is `src/dev/report.ts`.
   - `/__report` takes one POST per file: `/__report/<id>/<file>`.
   - With no in-game console in M0, §3.5's `/report` is Ctrl+Alt+F12 and `__game.report()`, and `/shot` is
     `__game.shot()`.
   - The reports and shots are gitignored; `bench/user-results/` is kept.
8. **`bench.html` is served at `/src/dev/bench.html`**, and the dev server redirects `/bench.html` to it.
   - It is cross-origin isolated (COOP and COEP on the page, the gallery it frames, and everything they request) for
     fine timers. Without that, Firefox's 1 ms resolution failed the extraction p99 gate.
   - It runs the release module when there is one, else the dev module, and records which one ran.
   - It asks the dev server for the scenario files, which simtool writes on first use.
9. **The browser runner is a Playwright spec gated by `BENCH=1`**, because it appends to `bench/history.jsonl`. The
   A/B uses both trees' `wasmdev` modules without simcheck, as the Node runner does.
10. **The calibration compares means only**, the same browser family and the same module.
11. **The night screenshot** sets the renderer's daylight to 0 for one frame, because M0's sim has no day cycle.
12. **The UPS test** measures up to three 3-s windows and needs one at 60 ± 1.5, so that a neighbour's burst on the
    shared host (the clock rebases rather than spiral, §3.3) cannot make it flaky.
13. **Files outside the brief's list**, each small and for the harness:
    - `scripts/free-port.ts`;
    - `scripts/dev-endpoints-plugin.ts` and its test;
    - one plugin line in `vite.config.ts`;
    - `playwright.release.config.ts` (free ports);
    - `biome.json` (excludes the endpoints' output);
    - `game/.gitignore`;
    - two npm scripts in `package.json` (`test:slow` and `bench:ab`, both named in §2.3 and §10.6);
    - `crates/sim/src/testkit*` and `crates/sim/tests/rules/*`.
14. **No other package's code needed an integration fix.** The C5 merge auto-resolved. `report.ts` reads the GPU name
    from the renderer's own context, because a probe context made headless Firefox warn.

## Open issues and follow-ups (by importance)

1. **Audio is not attached to the game page.** `main.ts` still has C7's `attachAudio(app)` commented out, and
   nothing in `src/app/` adapts the AudioSystem to the App. So the M0 build is silent: no menu music, no footsteps or
   mining sounds. The adapter would:
   - build the `AudioSystem` with the bank worklet's URL, prototypes.json's sound ids and its hash;
   - `preload()`, then `unlock()` on the first gesture (07 §18), and `playMenu()` at the menu;
   - `startGame(seed)` on a new world's generation, and `endGame()` on leaving it;
   - feed every packet to `onRenderPacket(decodePacket(bytes))`, and a HUD subscription of its own to `onHud`;
   - take the camera through `setView`, the UI's `sound` hook through `ui(name)`, and the game menu's pause through
     `setPaused`.

   That is more than a small integration fix (UI events, its own subscription, an e2e). It is a follow-up for C7
   with C5, or a small WP. The audio package itself passes its exit.
2. **C5's 22 proposed strings and 286 Factorio labels** await the text owner (`game/locale/en.sources.yaml`); the
   item groups' names belong in the data (08 §5.1 #28).
3. **C5's deferred finding 9**: craftable counts through intermediates need the sim's answer in a projection (the
