## Decisions and deviations

1. **Texture format: KTX2 XUBC7 → BC7 from M0** (10 §9.2). This replaces §6.13's WebP transport and "KTX2 UASTC in M3".
   - On a real 2048² page, XUBC7 at quality 90 is 1.02 MB against WebP q90's 0.52 MB. Its quality is far better: PSNR 43.6 against 38.6 dB, ΔE p99 3.7 against 11.6.
   - WebP's chroma subsampling greys small colour details and leaves orange halos round 16 px copper icons. Both are visible in the crops.
   - BC7 needs ¼ of WebP's RGBA8 GPU memory and bandwidth, and transcodes in about WebP's decode time (219 against 150 ms per High page here).
   - UASTC with RDO is 40% larger at lower quality, and ETC1S mushes the hatching. XUBC7 is new (Basis 2.50, August 2026), so encoder and transcoder are pinned together.
2. **Shadows: ETC1S → BC4** (0.5 B/px, 79 kB per Normal page). They are soft masks that the shader hatches, so ETC1S's error (35.6 dB) is ±1% of ink.
3. **Shared page layout:** the Normal page is level 1 of the High page. Rectangles sit on multiples of 8 px, so they map exactly onto every level and onto BC blocks.
   - The streamed High level 0 is a separate file, and the shader clamps the level of detail per layer until level 0 arrives.
   - This replaces v1's separate High and Normal atlases and frame tables, so **the sprite manifest goes to version 2** (§15.7): `resolutions` is gone, the single `anim` is now a `layers` list, and pages carry `group`, `files` and `bytes`. This is a contract change under IMPL-11. The manifest is JSON checked by the asset build, not a `schema/` record, so this report is its note.
4. **Render only what moves, made concrete** (10 §9.1). A3's DIRECTION §9 said the layer is rendered "with the static parts as holdout". Instead:
   - the base is rendered with the animated parts hidden;
   - each layer frame is the full inked frame cropped to R, with the static parts present, so the ink lines between moving and static parts come out right.
   - The composite check on the keypunch found 99.97% of pixels within ΔE 3, and the frames are identical to A3's full renders. A3's DIRECTION §9 is reconciled.
5. **The shadow pass renders at Normal resolution** and once per set. It is the costliest pass (46–56 µs per canvas pixel at High), and masks are stored at Normal anyway.
6. **Audio runtime: an AudioWorklet is needed** (10 §9.4), against A4 §6.2's native-node graph.
   - Native voices cost 0.53% of a host core each, against 0.14% in a worklet bank. The bank also halves sample memory (Int16) and removes per-note node creation.
   - The FDN worklet replaces every convolver: convolution cost 10 points of Era II, the FDN 2.6.
   - Tape and tremolo move into the bank: they cost 8 points of Era II as node chains.
7. **The engine runs at 32 kHz** (10 §9.4). That is −22% CPU and −33% sample memory; the music is dark by design, and A4 §7.1 says nothing above 7 kHz matters for the SFX. This is a taste risk the user must A/B (open issue 1). The fallback is 48 kHz with smaller voice caps.
8. **The audio budget:** ≤ 8% of one reference core on average and ≤ 15% in any second, music and SFX together, in every era; main thread ≤ 0.5%, every tick ≤ 2 ms; decoded samples ≤ 96 MB.
   - This is the brief's example, and Eras I and II meet it as measured. A4 had proposed ≤ 15/25%.
   - A4's fallback to recorded music now triggers above 15% on the user's machine (A4 said 40%).
9. **Decoded audio memory is a new budget.** A4's prototype decodes to Float32 at 48 kHz: the shipping pack is 283 MB, and the demo pack 730 MB for Era I alone. Int16 at 32 kHz brings Eras I + II to 89 MB and the whole shipping pack to 95 MB.
10. **The first piece of a game plays from the menu loop's cached recording** (it is identical every game, A4 §5.1), so Era I's 6.3 MB pack leaves the first frame's critical path.
11. **Data is not split by milestone or era:** all of `spec/data` is 54 kB with brotli (measured), so splitting is not worth a failure mode.
12. **The service worker is optional and progressive** (10 §9.7). This contradicts 07 §19.2's and 10 §8.2's unconditional "a service worker caches the build"; the edits are listed below.
13. **A new thread, the asset worker** (fetch with priorities, transcode, audio decode, a KTX2 cache), beside §3.1's. §3.1 itself is B1's, so the edit is listed below.
14. **No eviction of atlas pages during play,** as in Factorio. The budgets hold with margin in BC7: the slice 43 MB and the full game 183 MB at High. The RGBA8 fallback defaults to Normal.
15. **§15.7 and codex B1-r1 finding 14.**
    - One placement formula in device pixels, k = S ÷ P with S = `PX_PER_TILE` · zoom · dpr, with fixtures at dpr 1 and 2.
    - A generated 16-entry `dirIndex` per set, for `Direction` 0–15, and orientations for movers.
    - A lookup-contract table for every namespace (`entity/`, `greybox/`, `item/`, `object/`, `decor/`, `cliff/`, `resource/`, `town/`, `mover/`, `fx/`), and one specified position hash for variants the packet does not carry, so every peer draws the same picture.
16. **Deviation in scope: `design/asset-study/` is new** and not in my brief's write list. Measurement scripts belong in the repository (10 Appendix A's rule; A1 kept `design/engine-study/`). Outputs go to the existing gitignored `out/` directories.
17. **Deviation in scope: CLAUDE.md's toolchain** gained `basisu` 2.50 and the cmake venv, and `fonttools` and `brotli` in `gamedev-python` (pm/README rule 6).
18. **Sizes in §12.** My two M0 rows grew: *Asset pipeline v0* 1.5k/0.3k LOC and 1–2 days → 2.5k/0.6k, 2–3 (it gains the run-time loader); *Audio v0* 2k/0.5k, 2–3 → 2.8k/0.7k, 3–4 (the worklets). The **Total row (B1's) is now short by 1.8k/0.5k LOC and 2 days**.

## Open issues and follow-ups (by importance)

1. **The 32 kHz engine needs the user's ears.** Render A4's three demos at 32 kHz and let the user A/B them. If it is rejected, the engine runs at 48 kHz: about +22% CPU and +50% sample memory, which pushes Era II past 8% unless voice caps shrink.
2. **The audio budget is tight for Era II and over it for the takeoff.**
   - Era II reaches about 7.5% + 1% SFX only once the tape and tremolo inserts run inside the bank; that is an estimate, not measured.
   - The takeoff is 8.8% before SFX; M4 needs a 32-voice stream cap and pre-rendered layers.
   - The factor 2.4 for audio is extrapolated; `bench.html`'s audio page decides on the user's laptop.
3. **The takeoff's scheduler hitch:** single main-thread ticks of 23–34 ms (frame drops). M4 must spread note generation.
4. **The transcoder's size:** 361 kB brotli stock. A trimmed build (XUBC7 + ETC1S, six targets) needs Emscripten; try the podman Ubuntu 24.04 fallback in the asset WP.
5. **XUBC7 maturity and browser coverage.** The codec is two months old (pinned). BC7 availability on Firefox/macOS and Safari is untested here (BC3 and RGBA8 fallbacks exist); `bench.html` should log the WebGL extensions. Plan B is UASTC with RDO (+40% download).
