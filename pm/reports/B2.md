## Decisions and deviations

1. **Texture format: KTX2 XUBC7 → BC7 from M0** (10 §9.2). This replaces §6.13's WebP transport and "KTX2 UASTC in M3".
   - On a real 2048² page, XUBC7 at quality 90 is 1.02 MB against WebP q90's 0.52 MB. Its quality is far better: PSNR 43.6 against 38.6 dB, ΔE p99 3.7 against 11.6.
   - WebP's chroma subsampling greys small colour details and leaves orange halos round 16 px copper icons. Both are visible in the crops.
   - BC7 needs ¼ of WebP's RGBA8 GPU memory and bandwidth, and transcodes in about WebP's decode time (219 against 150 ms per High page here).
   - UASTC with RDO is 40% larger at lower quality, and ETC1S mushes the hatching. XUBC7 is new (Basis 2.50, August 2026), so encoder and transcoder are pinned together.
2. **Shadows: ETC1S → BC4** (0.5 B/px, 79 kB per Normal page). They are soft masks that the shader hatches, so ETC1S's error (35.6 dB) is ±1% of ink.
3. **Shared page layout:** the Normal page is level 1 of the High page. Rectangles sit on multiples of 8 px, so they map exactly onto every level and onto BC blocks.
   - The streamed High level 0 is a separate file, and the shader clamps the level of detail per layer until level 0 arrives.
   - This replaces v1's separate High and Normal atlases and frame tables, so **the sprite manifest goes to version 2** (§15.7): `resolutions` is gone, the single `anim` is now a `layers` list, and pages carry `group`, `files` and `bytes`. This is a contract change under IMPL-11. The manifest is JSON checked by the asset build, not a `schema/` record, so this report is its note.
4. **Render only what moves, made concrete** (10 §9.1). A3's DIRECTION §9 said the layer is rendered "with the static parts as holdout". Instead:
   - the base is rendered with the animated parts hidden;
   - each layer frame is the full inked frame cropped to R, with the static parts present, so the ink lines between moving and static parts come out right.
   - The composite check on the keypunch found 99.97% of pixels within ΔE 3, and the frames are identical to A3's full renders. A3's DIRECTION §9 is reconciled.
5. **The shadow pass renders at Normal resolution** and once per set. It is the costliest pass (46–56 µs per canvas pixel at High), and masks are stored at Normal anyway.
6. **Audio runtime: an AudioWorklet is needed** (10 §9.4), against A4 §6.2's native-node graph.
   - Native voices cost 0.53% of a host core each, against 0.14% in a worklet bank. The bank also halves sample memory (Int16) and removes per-note node creation.
   - The FDN worklet replaces every convolver: convolution cost 10 points of Era II, the FDN 2.6.
   - Tape and tremolo move into the bank: they cost 8 points of Era II as node chains.
7. **The engine runs at 32 kHz** (10 §9.4). That is −22% CPU and −33% sample memory; the music is dark by design, and A4 §7.1 says nothing above 7 kHz matters for the SFX. This is a taste risk the user must A/B (open issue 1). The fallback is 48 kHz with smaller voice caps.
8. **The audio budget:** ≤ 8% of one reference core on average and ≤ 15% in any second, music and SFX together, in every era; main thread ≤ 0.5%, every tick ≤ 2 ms; decoded samples ≤ 96 MB.
   - This is the brief's example, and Eras I and II meet it as measured. A4 had proposed ≤ 15/25%.
   - A4's fallback to recorded music now triggers above 15% on the user's machine (A4 said 40%).
9. **Decoded audio memory is a new budget.** A4's prototype decodes to Float32 at 48 kHz: the shipping pack is 283 MB, and the demo pack 730 MB for Era I alone. Int16 at 32 kHz brings Eras I + II to 89 MB and the whole shipping pack to 95 MB.
10. **The first piece of a game plays from the menu loop's cached recording** (it is identical every game, A4 §5.1), so Era I's 6.3 MB pack leaves the first frame's critical path.
11. **Data is not split by milestone or era:** all of `spec/data` is 54 kB with brotli (measured), so splitting is not worth a failure mode.
12. **The service worker is optional and progressive** (10 §9.7). This contradicts 07 §19.2's and 10 §8.2's unconditional "a service worker caches the build"; the edits are listed below.
13. **A new thread, the asset worker** (fetch with priorities, transcode, audio decode, a KTX2 cache), beside §3.1's. §3.1 itself is B1's, so the edit is listed below.
14. **No eviction of atlas pages during play,** as in Factorio. The budgets hold with margin in BC7: the slice 43 MB and the full game 183 MB at High. The RGBA8 fallback defaults to Normal.
15. **§15.7 and codex B1-r1 finding 14.**
    - One placement formula in device pixels, k = S ÷ P with S = `PX_PER_TILE` · zoom · dpr, with fixtures at dpr 1 and 2.
    - A generated 16-entry `dirIndex` per set, for `Direction` 0–15, and orientations for movers.
    - A lookup-contract table for every namespace (`entity/`, `greybox/`, `item/`, `object/`, `decor/`, `cliff/`, `resource/`, `town/`, `mover/`, `fx/`), and one specified position hash for variants the packet does not carry, so every peer draws the same picture.
16. **Deviation in scope: `design/asset-study/` is new** and not in my brief's write list. Measurement scripts belong in the repository (10 Appendix A's rule; A1 kept `design/engine-study/`). Outputs go to the existing gitignored `out/` directories.
17. **Deviation in scope: CLAUDE.md's toolchain** gained `basisu` 2.50 and the cmake venv, and `fonttools` and `brotli` in `gamedev-python` (pm/README rule 6).
18. **Sizes in §12.** My two M0 rows grew: *Asset pipeline v0* 1.5k/0.3k LOC and 1–2 days → 2.5k/0.6k, 2–3 (it gains the run-time loader); *Audio v0* 2k/0.5k, 2–3 → 2.8k/0.7k, 3–4 (the worklets). The **Total row (B1's) is now short by 1.8k/0.5k LOC and 2 days**.

## Review response (codex B2-r1)

The review (`pm/reviews/B2-r1.md`: 1 critical, 10 major, 2 minor) is answered below, finding by finding. New measurements are reproducible from `design/asset-study/` (commands at the end). **Status: 10 fixed; 3 fixed with a deferred hardware measurement (findings 4, 5 and 13); none rejected.** Two budgets are now reported as **not met** rather than claimed: Era II's and the takeoff's audio CPU, and version 1's 2-hour render target.

**1. Critical, §15.7 indexing: Fixed.**
- Every part of every set now carries an explicit index table: `axes`, `shape`, `map` (packet value → index) and `table` (row-major, the last axis fastest). Each entry is a list of frame ids, so a base cut into pieces is one entry, and impossible combinations are empty lists, drawn as the greybox.
- The axis vocabulary is closed and versioned (`axesVersion`): `dir`, `mask`, `shape`, `stage`, `band`, `light`, `orientation`, `frame`. One generated flatten function exists in TypeScript and Python, and nothing else computes ids.
- Drawing programs per `kind`:
  - `entity`: base and layers while `DYN.state` = 1, else idle; the light scaled by `DYN.light`; the shadow, with a `frame` axis for `animShadow`.
  - `inserter`: platform [dir], arm, open and closed hands with `pivot`, `length` and `shoulder`. θ is in turns from north, clockwise, as Factorio's orientation, with the shoulder and hand-point formulas.
  - `object`: tree [shape × 5 leaf states] with shadows [shape].
  - `resource`: [shape × band].
  - `mover`: rolling stock, vehicles and turrets [64 orientations]; agents [16 × 2 frames]; the character's three clips `idle` 8×4, `run` 8×16 (advanced by distance, `cycleTiles`) and `mine` 8×16, selected by `MOVERS.flags` bit 0 and velocity.
  - `icon`.
- Fixtures: `test/fixtures/sprites/manifest.json` (one set of every kind) and `cases.json` (packet inputs → expected draws), run by both the renderer's Vitest suite and the asset build's Python checks. They are now part of the M0 golden set's tiny atlas (§12 gate item 6, §15.10).
- Version checks: `version`, `axesVersion` and `prototypesHash`.
- The manifest example was rewritten (furnace with pieces, tree with shape × stage, icon).
- §15.4 now states the θ and orientation conventions and defines `MOVERS.flags` bit 0.
- One correction found on the way: trees have **5** leaf states (4 stages left down to bare; §4.4 already said "leaf stage 0–4"), not 4. §9.1, §9.9, the art direction and the model now say 5.

**2. Major, compositing: Fixed and verified on real Survey entities.**
- New scheme (§9.1): F₀ is rendered in full. A working set is drawn as F₀'s pieces outside every R (at most 4 rectangles per R, sub-rectangles of F₀'s single cell) plus the layer frame inside R, so each screen pixel is drawn exactly once (half-open rectangles). Layers whose rectangles intersect are merged (a build gate).
- `render/pieces_check.py` composites as the renderer will: premultiplied, bilinear, the level by scale, 6 scales 0.3–2.3, sub-pixel offsets, levels 0–1. Against drawing the full frame whole, on the keypunch, assembling machine 1, the electric drill, the steam engine (13 moving parts) and the research hall (96), the **maximum ΔE is 0.00 everywhere**, including with a 22%-opaque band across R's edge. Version 1's patch-over-base deviated by more than ΔE 1 on 0.6–3.6% of pixels, up to ΔE 19.2 (the translucent case: 3.6%).
- Ink coordinates: the ink's position-dependent fields (pen pressure, mottle, paper grain, hatching) are computed once per set in canvas coordinates and cropped. `render/ink_locality_check.py` shows inking R + 16 px is **bit-exact** against inking the whole frame (6 sets × 4 frames). With A3's per-call fields it would differ by up to 142/255.
- Inking F₀ only on its passes' bounding box + 16 px is bit-exact too, with nothing outside it, on every entity of the complete build (`--bbox`). It covers 27% of the canvas, which cuts ink time by about 3.7×.
- The layered frames still match A3's full renders within ΔE 1 on ≥ 99.97% of pixels.
- §9.5's composite check now tests the boundaries: the pieces against the whole frame at levels 0–2 and offsets (ΔE ≤ 0.5), crop ink against whole ink (≤ 1 step), full renders (ΔE ≤ 3 on 99.9%), and no intersecting layers.

**3. Major, GPU memory: Fixed.**
- `budget_model.py` v2 counts allocations:
  - 32-px cells (+31% for whole sprites, +42% for layer frames, measured on the complete build's rectangles), fill 0.874, pages rounded per group;
  - immutable storage of every level of each mode;
  - the terrain, the light and shadow targets, the quad and index buffers, LUTs, glyphs, chunk textures and the chart.
- No growth and no power-of-two capacity: each array is allocated **once at load** with the manifest's page count. Undownloaded High levels are committed and counted.
- Resolution modes (§6.13): High (levels 0–3), Normal (1–3; shadows at 16 px per tile, which the half-resolution shadow target cannot distinguish) and Low (2–3, for RGBA8).
- The chart was the hidden item: 4 kB per charted chunk is 128 MB at 30k chunks. It is now a CPU store of palette indices plus a fixed 43 MB GPU cache, drawing 2×2-chunk tiles when a view shows more than 4,096 chunks. §6.10 gets a pointer.
- Totals at 1920×1080, dpr 1 / dpr 2:

  | High, BC7 | 150 / 164 MB | 358 / 372 MB | 256 / 768 |
  | Normal, BC7 | 91 / 105 MB | 144 / 158 MB | 192 |
  | Normal, RGBA8 | 152 / 166 MB | **356 / 370 MB** | 192 |
  | Low, RGBA8 | 100 / 114 MB | 168 / 182 MB | 192 |

  Plus the browser's framebuffers: 24 / 95 MB, listed and not ours.
- Normal RGBA8 does not fit the full game, so the mode is chosen at load from the manifest's page counts: the highest that fits and allocates. RGBA8 gets Normal in the slice and Low in the full game.
- Eviction is a safety net (a page → layer table, LRU) if even Low fails.
- Context loss: wait for `webglcontextrestored`, else recreate the canvas and context once, else an error with Reload. Re-upload from the asset worker's KTX2 cache, Normal levels first.

**6. Major, audio interfaces: Fixed.**
- One absence rule, as §15.4's: an absent `AUDIO_WORKING` means none, and loops fade. Packets that do not arrive change nothing until 0.5 s of staleness.
- Activity: the audio module maps HUD watts to a = clamp(0.5 + 0.25·log₁₀(P/P_era), 0, 1), smoothed over 120 s, with P_era per era in `sounds.yaml`. This is A4 §5.1 rule 5, made explicit (§15.5's HUD row points to it).
- Sound ids: dense `u16` ids generated from `sounds.yaml` by the data stage, written to `prototypes.bin`/`.json`. The asset build's audio manifest is joined by name and checked against `prototypesHash`.

**7. Major, streaming contracts: Fixed.**
- Groups are per unlocking technology, merged up to 3 MB. The model: whole-tier groups would reach 5.7 MB (Report), while technology groups top out at 2.2 MB (13 groups).
- The build writes an exact byte inventory (`inventory.json`) and fails above 5 MB for `core` plus the first frame and above 3 MB for any other group.
- Priorities: missing Normal pages first. High level 0 takes at most 2 of the 6 connections while any Normal page is missing.
- Retries: backoff 1/2/4/8 s, then every 30 s, plus a hash check. Readiness is defined per page and per group; only `core` blocks, with a Retry screen.
- Late audio: a piece starts only when decoded, otherwise the silence is extended; late one-shots are dropped; loops start from the current snapshot. Late art keeps the current frame, variant, shape and stage.
- M1 tests a join and a load with no prefetch lead time.

**8. Major, worklet and autoplay failures: Fixed.**
- §9.4 specifies:
  - `AudioContext` creation, `resume()` and the menu loop's `play()` inside the first user activation;
  - rejected `play()` or a suspended context → a muted-speaker button that retries on the next gesture;
  - `resume()` on `visibilitychange` and `focus` (`suspended` or `interrupted`);
  - a missing `audioWorklet` (no secure context), `addModule()` rejection or `processorerror` → **reduced audio**: recorded pieces via a media element, native SFX with 8 voices and 4 loops.
- The processors never allocate in `process()`: a preallocated table of 2,048 notes, at most 10 messages per second, setup outside `process()`. The prototypes (`bank-worklet.js`, `sampler-worklet.js` with its preallocated queue, `fdn-worklet.js`) were checked: no allocation in `process()`.
- §12's Audio v0 exit now includes the failure paths with the worklet blocked and autoplay refused.

**9. Major, decoding contract: Fixed.**
- One decode path: `decodeAudioData` on an `OfflineAudioContext` at 32 kHz, at most 2 files at once (temporary Float32 < 8 MB), Int16 conversion sliced to 1 ms per frame.
- WebCodecs is dropped for now: it would need an Ogg demuxer, the OpusHead description and a resampler. It is optional later behind the same interface.
- Decode failures: retry, then that file's AAC version; two failures switch the pack.
- The Safari Ogg probe decides at run time.
- Loop points live on a canonical decoded timeline (32 kHz, after priming). The manifest stores each file's canonical length and a 64-sample start fingerprint, so AAC priming left in by a browser is found and trimmed. A Playwright test checks every loop seam of both packs.
- Samples are stored at 32 kHz whatever the context rate.
- Residency, measured (Int16, 32 kHz): all 12 instruments 94.6 MB (piano 54.6); Era I 79, II 65, takeoff 84, I ∪ II 89 MB. The build gate is 90 MB per adjacent era pair.

**10. Major, mip block isolation: Fixed.**
- Every frame is packed in a cell with an 8-px real-pixel gutter, positioned and sized on 32 px at level 0, so every 4×4 block down to level 3 belongs to one cell.
- Cost, measured on A3's 120 sprites (`texfmt/pack_alignment.py`): +31% sprite area, against +19% for 8-px cells and +2% for v1's gutters. The model includes it.
- **Verified** (`texfmt/cell_isolation_check.py`): a worst-case page of solid, strongly different cells, at levels 0–3, transcoded to BC7, ASTC 4×4, BC3, ETC2, BC4 and EAC R11. No texel of any level or format came out nearer a neighbour's colour; the worst error was 0.016, from quantisation. Version 1's packing failed at levels 2–3 in every format, with up to 7% of texels (ETC2, level 3) taking a neighbour's colour.
- §9.5 adds a build check: cells on 32 px with no overlap, then every page transcoded to every chain format, levels 0–3 decoded, and every frame's edge texels sampled with the renderer's filter. A texel reading another cell fails the build.
- Trim boxes are also rounded to 8 px of the canvas, so F₀, its pieces and the layer frames share one texel grid down to level 3 (§9.2 step 3).

**11. Major, night grading twice: Fixed.** §6.2 step 7 is now the only night grade (the light composite). Step 8's world overlays come after it and are never graded. Step 9 is only the colour-blind LUT, over everything. §9.8 says the same.
