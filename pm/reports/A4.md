# A4: Audio direction (music and SFX)
Status: done

## Summary

**Direction.** Process music played by the history of machine music. The full write-up is `design/audio/DIRECTION.md`.
- The soundtrack is calm, warm, slightly melancholic minimalism, built from rules rather than scored:
  - Eno's loops of unequal lengths;
  - Reich's tape phasing;
  - Riley's modules and time-lag accumulator;
  - Glass's additive and subtractive processes;
  - Pärt's tintinnabuli;
  - Nancarrow's tempo canons.
- Its instruments follow the Literature year:
  - 1945: acoustic (Steinway, strings, clarinet, harp);
  - 1957: tape, sine drones, organ, Risset bell;
  - 1983: FM;
  - 2000: samples and grains;
  - from 2017: the corpus.
- **Through-lines across the whole game:**
  - one motif, from the charter piece to the after-victory callback;
  - one piano;
  - harmony that brightens from D minor pentatonic to the harmonic series of D;
  - a performer automated by degrees: human rubato, then tape, MIDI, grains, and finally a player piano at superhuman speed. Nobody comments.
- **The one unique cue** (08 §2 rule 8) is a Nancarrow convergence canon at closure. The 1945 motif sounds in five voices, one per era, at tempo ratios 1 : 4/3 : 2 : 3 : 4, all landing on one D.
- **The takeoff** is a Risset eternal accelerando that doubles its tempo whenever research doubles (H #7). It is played on the piano and tuned to the overtone series in 5-limit just intonation.

**Engine: (c), a generative-first hybrid.**
- A WebAudio engine plays the rule sets live, from about 7 MB of CC0 samples, with generated reverbs and synthesized voices.
- The same code runs inside `OfflineAudioContext`. That produced the demos, and it would produce the menu loop and recorded fallbacks.
- **Measured:**
  - main thread: 0.1–0.6%;
  - audio thread on this host: 36% of a core (Era I), 53% (Era II), 76% (takeoff). That is roughly 20–42% on the reference laptop. DIRECTION §6.5 lists the cuts that reach ≤15% and ≤25%, chiefly an FDN reverb worklet instead of long convolution.
- **Variety.** A 12.6 h simulated game (`render/session.mjs`) plays about 200 segments, music 67% of the time, and repeats no piece except the deliberate callback.

**Deliverables:**
- `design/audio/DIRECTION.md`: style and atmosphere rationale, the per-era plan, adaptivity rules, the engine evaluation and architecture, the SFX palette and rules, mixing targets, the M1 build list, do/don't, the pipeline.
- `design/audio/LICENSES.md`: VCSL and VSCO 2 CE (CC0), pinned commits. GeneralUser GS is excluded for unclear provenance.
- **Demos**, each 2:25–2:27, Opus 128 kbps, −16 LUFS, ≤ −1 dBTP:
  - `design/audio/demos/paperwork-era1-1945.opus`;
  - `design/audio/demos/paperwork-era2-1962.opus`;
  - `design/audio/demos/paperwork-takeoff.opus`.
- **SFX:** `design/audio/demos/sfx/`, 15 prototypes in 38 files: UI click and hover, build ×3 sizes, deconstruct, mine ore, inserter, keypunch loop, belt loop, research complete, alert, error, footsteps on grass and concrete.
- **A listening page:** `design/audio/demos/index.html`, self-contained, ready to publish with the Opus and SFX files.
- **`design/audio/proto/`, reusable:**
  - the engine (JS modules);
  - three rule sets;
  - the conductor;
  - the offline renderer, Node dry run, realtime CPU benchmark and loop-seam test;
  - sample fetch and prep (full and shipping profiles);
  - SFX synthesis;
  - analysis, calibration, pack matching and mastering.

  The commands are in DIRECTION §12.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **10 §9.4 (music by music21/pretty_midi + FluidSynth/GeneralUser GS): replaced.** Music is composed at runtime by rule sets in a WebAudio engine, from CC0 multisamples.
   - GeneralUser GS is excluded: its author cannot vouch for every sample's origin, and the brief says nothing with unclear licensing goes in.
   - FluidR3 is clean but far below VCSL/VSCO quality.
   - music21 and pretty_midi are not needed.
2. **09 §9 ("one placeholder ambient track") and 10 §12 M1c ("2–3 music tracks"): two rule sets instead.** M1 ships the Era I and Era II rule sets, which cover the slice's Literature range to 1969, plus the conductor. Estimate: 7–9 agent-days for M1 audio in total (DIRECTION §10).
3. **New format details within 10 §9.4.** Opus stays primary with an AAC fallback.
   - Samples are decoded with `decodeAudioData`. Music fallbacks and the menu loop stream through a media element.
   - Loops use manifest loop points, never file boundaries.
4. **Proposed additions to `pm/DECISIONS-IMPL.md`** (the manager owns it):
   - (a) engine option (c), generative first, with offline-rendered fallbacks from the same code;
   - (b) CC0-only instrument samples (VCSL, VSCO 2 CE) at pinned commits; GeneralUser GS excluded;
   - (c) the music era is chosen by the Literature year when a piece starts;
   - (d) the closure cue is the convergence canon;
   - (e) the takeoff tempo tracks the doubling time (clamped to 3–90 s);
   - (f) after victory, the charter piece once more, played by the machine (08 P6's one callback);
   - (g) the music never reacts to alerts or danger.
5. **Proposed `CLAUDE.md` toolchain line** (outside my write scope): "Audio samples: `gamedev-python -I design/audio/proto/samples/fetch_samples.py` (CC0 VCSL + VSCO 2 CE, ~1 GB, into the gitignored `design/audio/out/`), then `prep_samples.py --profile full|ship`; the music engine renders in Chrome via `design/audio/proto/render/render.mjs`."
6. **No tools installed.** Everything ran on the existing toolchain: Node 24, Playwright with Chrome 156, gamedev-python (numpy, scipy, numba, soundfile, soxr, librosa, matplotlib), ffmpeg/libopus.
   - The samples (~1 GB raw) are in `design/audio/out/samples/raw/`, gitignored, with their licence files.
7. **Era plan beyond the brief's seed.** Era III starts in 1983 (DX7 and MIDI), not in the 1970s. The corpus era (2017 on) is added because at the competent pace it covers ~5½ h of play.

## Verification

- **Harmony.**
  - A symbolic check over every rendered note finds 0 sustained minor seconds or ninths below C5 in all three demos and in two game-length (5–6 min, transposed) Era I pieces.
  - Low-mid crowding: at most 4 sustained notes at MIDI 45–59, for 0% of the time; no intervals of a third or less below E3.
  - Scores were printed as chord names and read. That exposed three real bugs, all fixed:
    - a NaN in the voicing search froze the strings;
    - the top line was chained across rests;
    - the piece ended on B♭/D instead of Dm.
- **Balance.** Stem loudness is measured in the page (BS.1770, gated). `calibrate.py` converged every channel to within 0.6 LU of target. Examples:
  - Era I: piano −6.8 LU, clarinet melody −8.3, string sections −13.3, −14.2 and −15.3, bass −17.2.
  - Era II: marimbas −10.5, vibes −11.3, drone −15.0.
- **A sampler fault found and fixed.** VSCO 2's velocity layers differ by up to 29 dB, so the crossfade was dominated by forte layers even at pp. Layers are now normalized in prep, with designed steps, and all demos were re-rendered.
- **Form and dynamics.** LRA: Era I 7.7, Era II 9.7, takeoff 11.4 LU. Short-term loudness curves show each piece's arc (`design/audio/out/analysis/final-*.png`).
- **Spectrum.** Tilt above 500 Hz: −7.5, −8.9 and −6.7 dB/octave. The 2–5 kHz share is 1–4% for the music; the piano carries a −3 dB dip at 2.8 kHz.
- **Masters.** I −16.1, −16.0 and −16.0 LUFS; Opus true peak −1.2, −1.3 and −1.0 dBTP. The limiter reduces gain by more than 1 dB on ≤ 0.39% of samples.
- **SFX:**
  - K-weighted centroids: UI 0.74–1.05 kHz; machines 0.44–0.8; builds 0.16–0.35. Footsteps are the brightest, 1.9–2.3 kHz, at −40 LUFS-M.
  - The keypunch loop's crest factor is 15 dB.
  - Both loops are seamless as WAV, and after Opus decoding and looping in Chrome: decoded length exact, lag 0, seam step below the 99th-percentile step.
- **Packs.**
  - Shipping pack: 7.8 MB for 12 instruments, about 7.5 MB for the slice including SFX. The full pack is 47 MB.
  - After `packmatch.py`, an Era I render from the shipping pack matches the full pack within 0.1 dB per instrument and 0.1 LU overall.
- **CPU.** `render/bench.mjs`, live AudioContext, per-thread CPU from /proc:
  - audio thread 36%, 51–53% and 76% of a core;
  - without convolution: 35% (Era II) and 40% (takeoff);
  - main-thread scheduler 0.09–0.6%.
  - Offline: 1,450 notes created up front rendered at 0.67× real time, against 3.2× with lookahead; this is why lookahead is a rule.
- **Robustness.**
  - An exception in an offline tick used to stall rendering forever. The clock now records the error and resumes.
  - Notes due in the past are played if up to 50 ms late, otherwise dropped and counted.
  - Node dry runs validate every event of every rule set in milliseconds.

## Open issues and follow-ups

1. **Taste is unverified.** No one here can listen. The user should judge the three demos: `demos/index.html` puts them on one page with notes. The risky choices:
   - Era II's dark, marimba-warm balance (`air()` and the mallet velocities are the knobs);
   - the takeoff's just-intonation piano.
2. **The audio-thread budget on the reference laptop is extrapolated.** The audio WP must:
   - implement the cuts in DIRECTION §6.5: an FDN worklet for long tails, one ≤4 s convolver per era, no layer crossfade for fast notes, voice caps;
   - add the audio page to `bench.html` before M1 exits.
3. **Safari:** probe Opus decoding at startup and keep an AAC pack.
4. **Eras III, IV and IV′ are designed, not built.** The corpus era covers ~5½ h and needs the richest rules (M2–M3).
5. **Phase B:** align with A3's art direction, which is unfinished at the time of writing; its directions so far are mid-century and archival, compatible with this one. Agree the UI SFX material (card stock, bakelite) with the UI skin.
6. **The activity signal** (power over 60 s, log-scaled) needs calibration on real factories at M1.
7. **Optional, decide at M3:** "the corpus narrows as σ rises", a model-collapse echo that must stay inaudible to anyone who does not know it.
