# A3: Art direction
Status: done

## Summary

**Recommendation: "Survey".** The world is drawn as the survey the charter attaches. 3D models are rendered
headlessly, washed in three flat tones, inked and hatched. The ground is buff survey paper with topographic
conventions (woodland tint, water-lining, urban tint, stipple). The HUD is the paperwork of a 1945 federal site:
manila folders, index cards, printed labels and typed values, in Factorio's unchanged layout.

Information items are paper (light, with ink marks) and matter items are pigment (mid to dark). Working fires and
lamps leave a warm stain; at night the plate becomes a cyanotype that the lights colour back in. The style guide is
`design/art/DIRECTION.md`.

What exists:

- **Mocks** (`design/art/mocks/`, 1920×1080):
  - three directions at 1× and 0.3×: `kodachrome-*` (period realism), `survey-*` (first iteration) and `model-*`
    (clean diorama);
  - the final iteration: `survey-final-1x.png`, `survey-final-0.3x.png`, `survey-final-night.png` and
    `survey-final-eras.png` (the same pen across the arc);
  - contact sheets for the three rendered sets: `contact-kodachrome.png`, `contact-model.png` and
    `contact-survey-final.png` (every sprite with its shadow, every icon at 64, 32 and 16 px);
  - `compare-directions.png`: the four 1× mocks side by side.
- **Composition.** Every mock is composed from procedurally produced assets only: 29 entity scripts (18 of the data's
  entities, plus trees, rocks, decoratives, resource tiles and town buildings), 14 item models, numpy terrain and an
  SVG HUD.
- **Scene.** An hour-one Memo-tier Site next to the starting college town:
  - archive drills feed Keypunches;
  - a two-lane bus carries tokens and plates to gear and Memo assemblers, which feed 12 chained Seminars;
  - steam power runs on the lake, with wired poles, the researcher in a lab coat, the campus and the classical
    library beside its archive, and one idle furnace among working ones.
- **Pipeline** in `design/art/proto/`:
  - Blender rig, materials and parts library, entity scripts, renderers;
  - the ink pass, terrain, an engine-emulating compositor (10 §6.2 layer order), the HUD skin;
  - contact sheets, legibility metrics, budget estimate, night, era ladder and animation check.
- **How to run:** DIRECTION §13. In short: `render_all.sh survey 16`, then
  `gamedev-python design/art/proto/mock.py survey --tag final`.

## The directions and the critique

Each mock was viewed at 1×, 2× and 0.3× and measured with `proto/metrics.py` and `proto/budget.py`.

| | A Kodachrome (period realism) | C Site model (diorama) | **B Survey (recommended)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Look | PBR, procedural grime and weathering, olive photographic ground, Kodachrome grade | matte painted model, soft light, bright flocked ground, light modern UI | ink, three-band washes, hatched shadows, survey paper, form UI |
| Readability at 1× | good; glow sells state; belts match the grass's luminance (Weber 0.00), so they lean on their yellow rails | good, but low figure-ground: Seminar Weber −0.02, machines −0.26…+0.19 | best figure-ground: machines −0.25…−0.60 against paper; outlines keep silhouettes crisp |
| Legibility at 0.3× | readable, dark and busy | readable, bland | reads as an illustrated map; belts as ink threads |
| Information vs matter (icon groups, L\*) | 45 vs 31 (best split) | 49 vs 36; Records and Copper plate collapse under deuteranopia (4.5) | 45 vs 35 after the lightness rule and sel-out icons; worst cross pair under CVD 6.7 |
| Atmosphere and theme | serious, industrial, period; the closest to Factorio | cheerful, toy-like; wrong register for a dry, ominous arc | on-theme: "the attached survey", *Paperwork*, the logbook voice, mid-century technical plates |
| Distinctiveness from Factorio | low | medium (generic cosy builder) | high |
| Feasibility | hardest: realism exposes every shortcut and needs per-asset material work; 9.5 s per sprite | easy; similar render cost | rule-driven, so consistent across agents; 6.8 s per sprite plus about 1 s of ink |
| Runtime (WebP per sprite; shadow area) | 3.7 kB; 59k px | 3.4 kB; 74k px | 3.1 kB; 35k px (crisp, no soft tail) |
| Eras | natural (materials change) | weak (a toy datacenter) | one pen for every era; machines carry the era (eras sheet) |
| Image-model room | most | least | moderate: washes, paper and terrain textures only |
| Night and light | natural | natural | needed a concept; the cyanotype night is now one of its best images |

Verdict by direction:

- **Kodachrome is the viable fallback.** The same pipeline produces it by switching the style table. It is the
  closest to Factorio's tactile feel but the least distinctive, and the costliest to bring to Factorio-grade
  realism without texture sources.
- **The model direction failed on tone.** It is legible and cheap, but it says "cosy" and looks like many other
  games.
- **Survey won.** It wins on identity, theme, figure-ground, compression and consistency.

The final iteration took from the others:

- **Light from Kodachrome:** emitters render a light pass, become a warm stain by day and reveal colour at night,
  and burners smoke.
- **The lightness split from the metrics:** information L\* ≥ 84 with ink marks, matter ≤ 74, 20 L\* between
  same-hue pairs. This fixed the colour-vision collapse.
- **Line and UI refinements:**
  - organic shapes take silhouette lines only;
  - ink weight is used as hierarchy;
  - sel-out icon outlines;
  - a lighter belt deck so coal separates;
  - a filled-in-form HUD (Libre Franklin printed, Courier Prime typed);
  - an idle furnace beside working ones to show status contrast.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The look replaces 07 §23's "desaturated colour with a warm key light and grime"** and 10 §9.1's
   `materials.py` sentence. The brief made that line a candidate, not a given. The proposed spec edits are tabled in
   DIRECTION §15.
2. **Split light (07 §23, 10 §6.7).** The shading key comes from the west-south-west, (−0.62, −0.30, 0.73), so the
   south faces we see are lit. The shadow light comes from the west-north-west, (−0.80, 0.22, 0.56), so shadows
   still fall east-south-east as specified. A single north-west sun would put every front face in shadow.
3. **Icons are rendered from 3D item models** through the ink pass with sel-out outlines (`proto/render_icons.py`),
   not hand-authored SVG (10 §9.3). Reason: an item on a belt must be the same object as in the world, and one
   model source is cheaper and more consistent. SVG stays for UI glyphs and 9-slices.
4. **Extra render passes** beyond 10 §9.1: normal and aux (2×, 1 sample) for the ink pass; light for the glow.
5. **Belt chevron ridges**, a readability addition: direction reads in a still and at 0.5×.
6. **Interpretations of data lines.**
   - The Keypunch's "Burns fuel." became a stove-engine driving it by belt.
   - The Seminar's dome has a schoolhouse milk-glass globe that lights when working: a shaded bulb is invisible
     from 45°.
   - The staff socket is drawn empty (09 §2.4; dimming is a UI state).
   - The archive is bundled manila records on pallets.
7. **The brief's "typing pool" does not exist in the spec** (no hit in `design/`). Nothing was invented. The mocks
   use only real slice objects and the text canon's strings, for example the Keypunch description verbatim.
8. **Mock layout, not worldgen.** The starting archive sits south of the town rather than opposite the campus
   (01 §5.4 says "where possible") so that it is in frame next to its library. The 1× view is shifted 7 tiles east
   so the town clears the HUD column.
9. **The mocks are composed by a numpy emulation of 10 §6.2** (terrain, ground, belt items, one MAX shadow layer,
   y-sorted objects, high layer, light, post), because no engine exists yet. Inserter arms are posed 3D renders
   rather than 2D-rotated parts; at mock zoom the two are visually equivalent.
10. **UI fonts.** Libre Franklin and Courier Prime (both OFL), fetched by `proto/fetch_fonts.sh` into the gitignored
    `design/art/out/fonts`. Titillium Web stays the world-label font of 10 §6.11 unless the manager adopts the
    proposed change. Courier (1955) is acknowledged as a stand-in for a 1940s pica face.
11. **Proposed additions to `pm/DECISIONS-IMPL.md`** (manager-owned):
    - (a) the Survey direction and DIRECTION.md are binding for assets;
    - (b) the information/matter lightness rule;
    - (c) icons rendered from models through the ink pass;
    - (d) the day–night cyanotype LUT;
    - (e) the asset pipeline renders each model once per direction and only its animated layer per frame.

## Verification

- **Projection calibration** (10 §9.1). A unit cube rendered 64 px wide with a 64 px top face and a 64 px south face
  (Blender 5.2.2, `pixel_aspect_x = √2`).
- **Renders.** All ran on socket 1 (`taskset -c 8-15,24-31`), 16 threads, one Blender job at a time.

  | Set | Sprites | Wall time | Per sprite |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Kodachrome (2 passes) | 106 | 1,006 s | 9.5 s |
  | Survey (5 passes) | 120 | 819 s | 6.8 s, plus about 1 s of ink per sprite in numpy |
  | Icons | 25 per style | about 1 min | — |

  Composing a mock takes 30–90 s at 1× and 60–150 s at 0.3×.
- **Legibility** (`proto/metrics.py`; 8×8 Lab thumbnails over a belt deck, normal vision, deuteranopia and protanopia,
  Machado 2009).

  | | Closest pair | Closest info–matter pair (normal / worst CVD) | Info vs matter L\* |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Kodachrome | 4.6 (coal–wood) | 11.1 / 6.5 | 45 vs 31 |
  | Model | 7.1 | 10.3 / 4.2 | 49 vs 36 |
  | Survey, final | 7.7 | 8.7 / 6.7 | 45 vs 35 |

  Figure-ground (Weber, machines against the ground): kodachrome −0.21…+0.56 with belts at 0.00; model −0.26…+0.19;
  survey −0.25…−0.60.
- **Budget** (`proto/budget.py`; shelf-packed 2048² pages, WebP q90, mean areas extrapolated to 10 §9.1's frame
  counts). Survey:
  - mean colour area 28k px; 3.1 kB WebP per sprite;
  - full game as full-frame sprites: 51 colour pages, about 1.1 GB at High with mips, over 10 §6.13's 768 MB;
  - with animated-layer frames: about 17 pages, about 380 MB;
  - slice: 12.7 pages full-frame, about 285 MB (over 256 MB); about 100 MB with layers;
  - download about 18 MB colour for the full game.
  - All three styles land within 10 % of each other on colour. They differ on shadows (clipped and at Normal
    resolution, per DIRECTION §4.2) and on compression.
- **Animation** (`extras.py anim survey keypunch`): 16 frames, GIF in `design/art/out/anim/`. Frame registration is
  clean: only the animated parts change. The moving area is 0.5 % of the canvas, too subtle, which is why
  DIRECTION §9 sets a 3 % rule.
- **Screenshots reviewed** (every PNG was read):
  - mocks at 1× and 0.3×, with 2× crops of each style;
  - the HUD at 2× for each skin;
  - icon sheets on dark and paper grounds at 64, 32 and 16 px;
  - the contact sheets, the night and era images, and the animation strip.
  - Defects found and fixed on the way:
    - grass seams (non-periodic `ndi.zoom`);
    - tile-staircase shores (replaced by soft field blending);
    - navy belts (sky reflection);
    - Seminar "brown jelly" glass;
    - missing `⇒` glyph;
    - overflowing typewriter text;
    - bubble-like smoke;
    - a glow layer lost in the sprite cache;
    - a per-frame, process-random ink seed (crc32 now);
    - coal invisible on the belt deck.
- **Syntax:** `gamedev-python -m py_compile` passes on every prototype script.

## Open issues and follow-ups

1. **The recommendation is a taste call with real risks; the user should see `compare-directions.png` before it is
   locked.**
   - (a) A Factorio veteran may miss tactile realism. The glows, smoke and cyanotype night mitigate this;
     Kodachrome remains a one-table switch.
   - (b) A light paper world for 12-hour sessions: the paper is buff (L\* 90), not white, the night cycle varies it,
     and a "paper tone" setting would be cheap. This is untested with players.
   - (c) Automatic inking on complex geometry still produces stray creases on some shapes, so line thresholds need
     per-family tuning.
2. **Pipeline throughput.** The prototype rebuilds the scene for every frame: about 11 hours for a 6,000-frame set
   on 16 threads. The asset WP must build each model once per direction and render only its animated layer per
   frame (10 §9.1 #4) to reach the spec's 2 hours.
3. **GPU budget.** Full-frame animation sheets exceed 10 §6.13. Animated layers, shadows at Normal resolution with
   clipped tails, and the planned M3 KTX2 compression bring the set within budget (DIRECTION §13.6).
4. **Animation readability.** The prototype Keypunch moves 0.5 % of its pixels. Its moving parts (card bed,
   flywheel) must be enlarged to the 3 % rule before production; check every machine likewise.
5. **Not yet produced:**
   - idle variants beyond the furnace;
   - four directions for asymmetric machines (drills and the pump have them);
   - red and blue belt tiers;
   - leaf stages;
   - town skins 2–5;
   - non-temperate biome washes;
   - the other 86 of the data's 104 entities.
6. **The UI skin is mocked on the HUD only.** Large windows (inventory, crafting, technology screen) must be tried
   for legibility and fatigue before M1's UI work.
7. **Night as gameplay.** The cyanotype makes lamps the way to see colour at night, so it needs a feel test (I §6
   style).
8. **Spec edits for the manager** (DIRECTION §15) and the DECISIONS-IMPL entries in deviation 11.
9. **Repository size.** The mocks total 32 MB after lossless oxipng. Commit them as they are, keep only the final
   set plus `compare-directions.png`, or move them to an artifact store.
