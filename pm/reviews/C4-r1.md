6. **Major — Context recovery can make streamed art permanently unusable.** [app/world-view.ts:135](/home/mewse/browsergame/.claude/worktrees/agent-a744e48a1a5a7400b/game/src/app/world-view.ts:135)  
   Recovery probes formats and chooses a mode again, but only calls the existing loader’s `replay()`. Its formats and skipped levels remain those selected initially. Example: Low initially skips colour level 1; recovery successfully allocates High, but replay still omits level 1, so those pages never become resident. A format change instead triggers an upload-format exception. **Fix:** renegotiate loader requests after recovery and reject responses belonging to the previous upload epoch.
