1. **Critical — Restart can fork the action history.** [DESIGN.md §6.9](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:616). The first reconnecting client may have received a shorter suffix than another client. Adopting that suffix and immediately resuming can assign different actions to ticks already simulated elsewhere. **Fix:** durably commit closures before broadcasting, or explicitly rewind every client to one persisted checkpoint under a new room generation. Validate that generation during resume.

2. **Major — Adaptive stamping can reverse a player’s actions.** [DESIGN.md §3.4](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:225). Margin reductions or clock corrections can produce `start_walking@100`, followed by `stop_walking@99`. The relay accepts both; the character keeps walking. A read-only PoC probe reproduced this. **Fix:** enforce nondecreasing assigned ticks per player, preserve sequence order within a tick, and reconcile prediction against acknowledged assignments.

3. **Major — Reconnect lacks reliable action identity and input fencing.** [DESIGN.md §§2.6, 3.2](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:147). Accepted actions whose acknowledgements disappear have unspecified retry semantics. A queued future start can also execute after `release_inputs`. **Fix:** use session-qualified action IDs, deduplication, explicit acceptance watermarks, and an input generation that invalidates queued held-input changes on disconnect. Start replacement connections independently of the old socket’s close event: browser `close()` drains queued sends first. [WebSocket standard](https://websockets.spec.whatwg.org/#dom-websocket-close).

4. **Major — Digest timeout can prevent comparison indefinitely.** [DESIGN.md §5.1](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:433). Clients may remain 60 seconds behind, but checkpoints expire after five seconds. Two clients consistently six seconds apart never compare matching checkpoints in the PoC. **Fix:** retain checkpoint results through the maximum supported lag and compare late reports against them; define expected membership at the checkpoint tick. Also implement the advertised epoch checks and restrict donors to the winning group—the PoC currently does neither.

5. **Major — Snapshot digest lineage is missing from the mandatory contract.** [DESIGN.md §7, S7–S9](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:627). A rolling digest depends on history, so loading identical world state cannot reconstruct it. The PoC saves `rolling`, but the production snapshot contract does not require this. **Fix:** checkpoint the rolling seed, slice schedule/cursor, and lineage metadata explicitly. D18 should compare full canonical state as well as heuristic traces; rotating samples cannot guarantee detection of every transient divergence.

6. **Major — Empty-room upgrades replay old actions under new rules.** [DESIGN.md §6.3](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:527). Migrating the cached snapshot and then replaying its remaining log with a new build can change the resulting factory or encounter incompatible actions. **Fix:** replay through the persisted horizon using the old build, migrate that resulting state, then atomically publish a new checkpoint and version pin. Retain assets for builds still serving active rooms.

7. **Major — Clock transitions are underspecified.** [DESIGN.md §§3.1, 3.4, 3.6](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:183). The fixed `t0 + kT` mapping and client extrapolation need explicit handling for pause, speed changes, sleep, and relay stalls. Otherwise paused clients stamp into the future or resume with obsolete playout deadlines. **Fix:** publish clock generations with tick, speed and paused state; rebase mappings and invalidate timing samples on transitions. Close required snapshot markers before activating “pause while joining.”

8. **Major — Queues and dependency retention are unbounded.** [DESIGN.md §§3.3, 6.5](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:210). Inbound limits do not bound relay outbound queues, net-worker-to-simulation queues, or retained logs during prolonged transfers. A slow consumer or trusted-client bug can exhaust memory. Blob retention across replay and restart is also unspecified. **Fix:** define byte high-water marks, transfer deadlines, room-wide budgets and disconnect/resnapshot behavior. Persist blobs while any retained closure references them.

9. **Major — Wire limits contradict their field widths.** [DESIGN.md §3.2](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:190); [poc/protocol.mjs](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/poc/protocol.mjs:40). Read-only probes confirmed that a 65,536-byte action decodes as zero bytes, and 65,536 closure entries decode as an empty batch while advancing the horizon. **Fix:** cap payloads at 65,535 bytes or widen lengths; split resume batches within entry and byte limits. Reject malformed lengths and noncontiguous ranges before advancing the horizon.

10. **Major — Snapshot pacing can stop progress at low rates.** [DESIGN.md §4.3](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:382); [poc/client-core.mjs, `donate`](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/poc/client-core.mjs:453). Token capacity is `rate × 0.1`, but sending requires a whole 64 KiB chunk. Below approximately 640 KiB/s, a fixed-rate bucket can never send; the relay has the same defect. **Fix:** allow capacity of at least one complete message and pace smaller fragments appropriate to slow uplinks. Test sustained 2 Mbit/s operation.

11. **Major — Partial-snapshot publication needs a complete transaction.** [DESIGN.md §§4.1–4.3](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:360). The PoC counts received messages rather than unique chunks, so duplicates can prematurely mark a snapshot complete. The design lacks a precise manifest and timeout protocol. **Fix:** bind tick, build, lineage, sizes and hash in an immutable manifest; validate bounds, count unique chunks, and publish atomically after verification. Capture all mutable state at the marker, including cold data, timers and pending worldgen intent.

12. **Major — Position fallback can target a replacement entity.** [DESIGN.md §3.3; §7 S5](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:210). A stale handle followed by matching position/prototype can resolve to another player’s newly placed replacement. Ordinary validation can succeed, contrary to the stated failure behavior. **Fix:** include the expected entity serial and reject identity mismatches. Refer to predicted creations through deterministic creation-action IDs; reserve coordinate targeting for actions explicitly defined that way.

13. **Major — M0 lacks a state-replacement protocol for consumers.** [DESIGN.md §7, S9–S11](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:627); [10-architecture.md §3.4](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/spec/10-architecture.md:298). Swapping workers leaves unspecified how render mirrors, subscriptions, pending queries and prediction discard the previous world. Version counters can coincide despite different contents. **Fix:** add a state generation, full view invalidation, subscription rebinding and stale-response rejection. Explicitly suppress historical sounds and feedback during replay/catch-up.

14. **Major — A second worker does not guarantee repair convergence.** [DESIGN.md §5.2](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:444). Memory is the only specified foreground-fallback trigger. CPU contention can leave the shadow at or below 60 UPS indefinitely while both worlds consume resources. **Fix:** measure catch-up rate under concurrent load, require positive progress, and automatically switch to foreground repair or a coordinated pause when progress stalls. Bound repeated repair attempts.

15. **Major — Evidence overstates completed behavior.** [DESIGN.md §12](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:817); [pm/reports/A2.md](/home/mewse/browsergame/pm/reports/A2.md). Digest agreement can pass while a slow or paced joiner never becomes live; paced repair remained unfinished. Assigned-tick agreement does not establish prediction exactness, and sleeping to emulate tick cost does not establish real concurrent CPU capacity. **Fix:** require completed joins, completed swaps, post-fault full-state agreement and real-engine resource measurements before declaring these paths validated.

16. **Minor — Browser guarantees need qualification.** [DESIGN.md §§2.1–2.2, 3.6](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:94). HTTPS availability does not guarantee WebSocket permission. The categorical absence of WebTransport TCP fallback needs revision against current [WebKit documentation](https://webkit.org/blog/17862/webkit-features-for-safari-26-4/#webtransport). Safari background behavior is documented: timer throttling, iOS suspension and macOS App Nap. [WebKit power guidance](https://webkit.org/blog/8970/how-web-content-can-affect-power-usage/). **Fix:** maintain a version-specific capability matrix and test release Safari alongside Chrome and Firefox; treat background execution as best effort.

17. **Minor — Delta repair is presented as cheaper to prepare than it is.** [DESIGN.md §§5.2, 7](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/multiplayer/DESIGN.md:444). Rotating hashes from different ticks cannot supply coherent page hashes at marker T, so delta repair does require additional scanning and snapshot coordination. **Fix:** defer mandatory page-layout commitments and the Cloudflare adapter until full-snapshot recovery works. Preserve canonical serialization and complete checkpoints from M0.

Architecture: relay-sequenced lockstep over WSS fits the constraints.  
Readiness: ordering, recovery and checkpoint contracts need revision.  
M0: retain the core seams, with the additions above.
