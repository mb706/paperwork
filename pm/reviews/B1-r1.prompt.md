You are a principal engine architect doing an adversarial review of the engine spec for the browser game *Paperwork*. It is a faithful Factorio 2.0-like:
- the simulation is deterministic, in Rust→WASM, in a Web Worker, at 60 UPS;
- TypeScript does the UI, the WebGL2 renderer, networking and the data stage;
- multiplayer is relay-sequenced lockstep (chapter 11).

M0 coding starts from this spec. Several agents will build the sim crate, the renderer, the UI and the asset pipeline **in parallel against the contracts in 10 §15**, without talking to each other. Your job is to find what would make that fail.

Read:
- `design/spec/10-architecture.md`, version 2. Focus on: the "Changes in v2" table, §0–§4, §8, §10–§12, §15 and Appendix A. Ignore §9 and the loading/streaming stub; another agent is rewriting them right now.
- `design/spec/11-multiplayer.md`, especially §7.
- `pm/DECISIONS-IMPL.md` (binding implementation decisions).
- `pm/reports/B1.md` (what changed and why).
- The engine study `pm/reports/A1.md` and its code in `design/engine-study/`, as needed.

Find:
1. **Contract defects in §15:** any layout, field width, endianness, ownership or versioning rule that is ambiguous, inconsistent between sections, or impossible to implement. Look for places where two parallel agents could legitimately implement incompatible things.
2. **Determinism holes:** in D1–D23, in the save container, the closure pipeline, worldgen, ordering, and the digest lineage.
3. **Performance traps:** in the render-packet path, the projections and subscriptions, and the scheduler.
4. **Contradictions:** between 10 and 11, between 10 and `pm/DECISIONS-IMPL.md`, and within 10. Watch especially for leftovers that still assume a TypeScript simulation.
5. **The M0 scope and exit criteria in §12:** are they realistic and testable? What is missing for a parallel start (stubs, fakes, a golden data set)?
6. **Over-engineering** that M0 does not need and that slows it down.

Output: a prioritized list of findings, at most 2,000 words. Tag each Critical, Major or Minor, and give the section, the problem, why it matters, and a concrete fix. End with a three-line verdict on whether M0 can start in parallel after the fixes. Do not modify any files.
