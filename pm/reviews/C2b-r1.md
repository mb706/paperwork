1. **Major — Worldgen identity omits a generation-affecting setting.** [worldgen/lib.rs:51](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/worldgen/src/lib.rs:51), [context.rs:375](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/context.rs:375). Worlds differing only in `climate_compass` receive identical hashes, although their generated climate—and potentially tiles—differs. In WASM, a compass-off answer was accepted by a compass-on world; its state hash then differed from inline generation. Structural validation cannot establish that supplied bytes match the world’s generator. **Fix:** include every generation-affecting setting and the generator version in the identity; update §15.9 accordingly, and test cross-setting answers plus worker-answer regeneration checks under `simcheck`.

2. **Major — Commit scheduling can loop forever at tick exhaustion.** [regen.rs:225](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/regen.rs:225). At tick `u32::MAX − 30`, crossing a chunk boundary requests 15 chunks. Eight occupy the final deadline; the ninth repeatedly executes `due.saturating_add(1)`, which never advances. A WASM probe loaded this state successfully and timed out inside the walking tick. **Fix:** use checked deadline arithmetic and define a finite exhaustion policy before modifying the schedule. Test nine requests at the final available deadline.

3. **Major — Load validation accepts state that the new systems immediately trap on.** [validate.rs:125](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/validate.rs:125), [validate.rs:143](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/validate.rs:143). Three independently confirmed WASM cases loaded successfully and trapped on the next empty tick: crafting recipe `65534` indexes beyond the recipe table; `MiningMode::WorldObject` reaches `unreachable!`; a pending request for an already-generated chunk fails the commit assertion. These remain gaps after C2a-r1’s validation fixes. **Fix:** validate queue recipes, positive counts and progress invariants, supported mining states, and pending requests’ surfaces, disjointness from `GENERATED`, and eight-per-deadline bound before installing the world.

4. **Major — Resource settings permit astronomical generation work and overflowing arithmetic.** [worldgen/lib.rs:199](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/worldgen/src/lib.rs:199), [worldgen/lib.rs:301](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/worldgen/src/lib.rs:301). `size = 1e30` passes `gen_init`; generating one chunk enumerates an enormous two-dimensional cell range. The probe timed out. Still larger sizes saturate the float-to-`i64` conversion, after which `+ 1` overflows in checked builds while release arithmetic wraps. Settings retained in a save can reach this through lazy regeneration. **Fix:** validate finite, bounded settings at new-world, generator-init and load boundaries; use checked coordinate arithmetic and enforce a bounded cell-search extent.

5. **Major — A fallible build becomes a panic instead of a refusal.** [player_actions.rs:267](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/rules/player_actions.rs:267), [player_actions.rs:280](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/rules/player_actions.rs:280). A validly loaded state with `next_serial = u32::MAX` traps when an otherwise valid chest build calls `create_entity`. For a pristine footprint, `make_modified` has already changed saved state before creation fails. Joining another player has a similar exhaustion `expect` at [system.rs:55](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/rules/system.rs:55). **Fix:** check creation resources before any mutation and propagate a structured failure; make the complete build operation transactional.

6. **Major — Fast transfer fails to replenish the cursor.** [player_actions.rs:543](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/rules/player_actions.rs:543). Successful insertion subtracts from the hand, clears it when empty, and returns. With 100 iron plates held and another 100 available in the main inventory, transferring into a chest leaves an empty hand. The corrected controls spec, I §3.8, requires the cursor to refill from inventory. **Fix:** share a replenishment helper with building and test full and partial fast transfers, including origin-slot handling and inventory-version changes.

7. **Major — Refused mining actions can force expensive distant regeneration.** [player_actions.rs:157](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/rules/player_actions.rs:157). `resource_at` runs before reach validation and regenerates uncached pristine chunks. On a loaded explored world, a player can target thousands of distinct distant chunks despite having no ability to mine them. A 16,016-byte closure containing 1,000 such attempts took approximately **281 ms** in WASM; the idle comparison took approximately 0.2 ms. Cache eviction permits repeated attacks. **Fix:** perform the cheap reach rejection before terrain access, and add a distant-action cost regression test. Apply the same principle to the tile-mining branch.

8. **Minor — Default starting iron is substantially below the designed opening.** [worldgen/lib.rs:291](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/worldgen/src/lib.rs:291). Radius 7 and base richness 1,500 with quadratic falloff produce roughly 115,000 ore: the four tested seeds yielded **114,239–118,416 ore across 156 tiles**. [01-world.md:115](/home/mewse/browsergame/design/spec/01-world.md:115) specifies 600,000 ±20% across approximately 600 tiles. The placement distance is plausible, but this deposit would exhaust roughly five times earlier. Full worldgen guarantees are deferred, so this is a calibration concern rather than a missing-town finding. **Fix:** calibrate existing resource parameters against the opening’s numbers and freeze expected ranges across seeds.

9. **Minor — TestSequencer masks send-order and identity errors.** [sequencer.rs:41](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/testkit/sequencer.rs:41), [sequencer.rs:79](/home/mewse/browsergame/game/crates/sim/src/testkit/sequencer.rs:79). Sequences are assigned when closures are produced. Queue A for tick 10, then B for tick 5: B receives the earlier sequence, hiding the send-order violation D21 should expose. Creation IDs also cannot be obtained when actions are queued. Despite its documentation, `TestSequencer` itself does not track or stamp input generations; `Scenario` supplies them from the current World. **Fix:** assign and return identities at enqueue time, enforce per-player tick ordering, and track generations from loaded metadata and system actions.
