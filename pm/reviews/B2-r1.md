1. **[Critical] §15.7 — Required indexing remains undefined.** The manifest lacks explicit layouts for mover orientations, character clips, inserter parts/pivots, multi-state signals and animated shadows. Tree shape × leaf stage and resource shape × depletion band have no flattening rule; shadows always select `f=0`. Parallel packages must invent conventions. **Fix:** freeze explicit axes, selectors and named parts in a shared schema, with representative fixtures and manifest/prototype version checks.

2. **[Major] §§9.1, 9.5 — Replacement patches are incorrectly composited source-over.** Cropped full frames contain static pixels already present in the base. Drawing them again doubles translucent washes and antialiased edges: repeating 22% glass produces 39.2% opacity. Intersecting animation patches can also overwrite another part’s pose. The keypunch check permits local ΔE up to 19.1. **Fix:** exclude replacement regions from the base, merge intersecting animations, preserve common ink coordinates/halos, and test boundaries and translucent geometry locally.

3. **[Major] §§6.13, 9.6–9.7 — GPU memory is substantially understated.** `budget_model.py` counts content pixels, omitting packing slack, group rounding, array capacity, growth overlap and terrain/render targets. For example, power-of-two growth turns 33 colour pages into capacity 64: Normal RGBA8 colour alone consumes **336 MiB**, exceeding the entire 192 MB budget. Undownloaded High levels still occupy immutable storage. **Fix:** model actual allocations and peaks; bound capacity and permit eviction. Recovery requires a restored or successfully recreated context.

4. **[Major] §§9.4, 11–12 — Audio budget compliance is unproven and internally contradicted.** Era II’s estimate is 7.5% + 1% SFX = **8.5%**; takeoff is 8.8% before SFX. The 7.0% measurement removes inserts rather than implementing them inside the bank. Twenty-second excerpts establish neither whole-piece averages nor one-second maxima, and ×2.4 remains extrapolated. Takeoff’s scheduler also reaches 23–34 ms against 2 ms. **Fix:** measure the complete graph with SFX on the reference laptop and reserve margin. `bench.html` cannot directly reproduce `/proc` CPU accounting.

5. **[Major] §9.2; change row 50 — Browser compression coverage is overstated.** UASTC/XUBC7 transcoding is valid ([Basis documentation](https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal)). Hardware/backend evidence supports expecting BC7 on [Windows](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/overviews-direct3d-11-devices-downlevel-intro), [Intel/Mesa](https://docs.mesa3d.org/relnotes/10.3.html) and [Chrome/Metal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/angle/main/src/libANGLE/renderer/metal/mtl_format_table_autogen.mm), but [BPTC exposure remains optional](https://registry.khronos.org/webgl/extensions/EXT_texture_compression_bptc/). Firefox/macOS needs measurement. **Fix:** probe and upload-test BPTC, ASTC, S3TC and RGTC independently; add ETC2 RGBA before RGBA8, retain EAC/R8 shadows, and validate fallback quality/performance. RGBA8/R8 need uncompressed upload calls. SwiftShader and Firefox without WebGL2 do not establish hardware coverage.

6. **[Major] §15.6 against §§15.4–15.5 — Audio interfaces disagree.** An absent `AUDIO_WORKING` snapshot means “none” in §15.4 but “unchanged” in §15.6. HUD activity is watts, while the conductor expects normalized 0–1 activity. Assignment of `u16 sound` IDs and their binding to the audio manifest are unstated. **Fix:** define one absence/muting rule, explicit power-to-activity normalization, and generated sound IDs shared by simulation and audio, with build/version checks.

7. **[Major] §9.7 — Stage limits and late arrivals lack enforceable contracts.** The model totals art without packing actual tier groups, so it cannot establish ≤3 MB per unlock. Slow networks and multiplayer joins can eliminate prefetch lead time; High requests can compete with missing Normal pages. **Fix:** generate exact dependency/byte inventories, prioritize essential Normal assets, and specify retries and readiness. Keep an available music piece or silence until the next era is ready; discard expired one-shots and resume loops from current snapshots. Late art must preserve current phase and variant.

8. **[Major] §9.4 — Worklet and autoplay failures need explicit handling.** Worklets require a [secure context](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/#AudioWorklet) and bounded work each render quantum. The prototype’s `q.splice()` allocates inside `process()`, risking [audio-thread garbage collection](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/audio-worklet-design-pattern/). **Fix:** use a preallocated bounded queue; handle module/processor failure with recorded music and a small native SFX fallback. Create/resume the context and start media within user activation, handle rejected `play()`, and check suspension on return. [Creating a context alone does not guarantee playback](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay/).

9. **[Major] §9.4 — Codec probes do not complete the decoding contract.** Safari’s Ogg support has [browser/OS requirements](https://webkit.org/blog/16574/webkit-features-in-safari-18-4/). WebCodecs consumes [encoded chunks](https://www.w3.org/TR/webcodecs/#audiodecoder), requiring container demuxing, decoder configuration and explicit resampling. **Fix:** check actual decoder support and fall back on decode failure. Specify loop points in a canonical decoded timeline, including priming/padding conversion; manifest points alone cannot guarantee AAC seams. Test decoded AAC loops. Budget temporary Float32 buffers; switching to 48 kHz increases sample residency independently of voice caps.

10. **[Major] §§9.2, 15.7 — Eight-pixel alignment does not isolate every mip’s compression blocks.** At level 3 it becomes one-pixel alignment and a one-pixel gutter; a 4×4 block can contain neighbouring sprites, contaminating ink and washes. **Fix:** use padded, block-aligned cells through the lowest mip—32-pixel alignment at High for level 3—and validate compressed mip/filter boundaries for every target format.

11. **[Major] §§6.2, 9.8 — Night grading is specified twice.** §9.8’s light composite already applies the cyanotype, while §6.2 step 9 requests another night LUT. Implementing both regrades colours and lights. **Fix:** assign night grading to one pass and specify whether overlays precede or follow it.

12. **[Minor] §9.7; change row 52 — Hosting guarantees need qualification.** Optional service workers and cloning `WebAssembly.Module` to dedicated workers are sound. However, `compileStreaming` rejects incorrect Wasm MIME types. **Fix:** add an `arrayBuffer`/`compile` fallback for that case; clone modules outside transfer lists and avoid guaranteeing compilation reuse. These distinctions follow the [Wasm Web API](https://webassembly.github.io/spec/web-api/index.html). Specify HTTPS/localhost hosting; `file://` generally fails module-fetch requirements. Promise offline access only to cached content.

13. **[Minor] §§9.6, 11–12 — Render acceptance overclaims the evidence.** The estimated 2.4-hour render already exceeds two hours, and the model omits per-frame character/radar shadows. Packing/encoding adds build time. **Fix:** reconcile the target and measure complete builds. Separately, §6.12’s full-game frame times require real UHD-class measurements.

Verdict: revise before freezing the M0 contracts.  
Survey and generative audio remain viable; budget compliance is unproven.
