Paperwork

Audio direction demos. Information is physical.

The music is process music played by the history of machine music. Each piece below is a render of the game's own engine (rules, not a fixed score), so in play every piece is different. Mastered to −16 LUFS.

Music

1945: Relays and tubes

Era I, the charter piece · piano, strings, clarinet, harp, glockenspiel · 2:25

The motif is stated once on the piano, then five piano loops of unequal lengths take it apart. They never line up the same way twice. The strings voice-lead under a pentatonic field. A clarinet melody in Pärt's tintinnabuli manner follows, with harp, then a climax in the violins and a quiet coda.

1962: Tape and oscillators

Era II · marimba tape loops, vibraphone, organ, sine drones, tape · 2:26

A marimba loop is built up note by note. A second copy runs 0.4% fast, so it drifts out of phase and, being tape, slightly sharp. Organ figures pile up on a tape echo over a just-intonation sine drone. Two Risset bells sound, and the 1945 motif returns on tape at half speed.

Takeoff

Era V · the loop closes, then the takeoff · 2:27

The 1945 motif in five voices, one per era, at tempi 1 : 4/3 : 2 : 3 : 4. All five arrive on one D at the same instant: the only moment every era sounds together. Then an accelerando without end on the piano, tuned to the harmonic series of D, which doubles in tempo whenever research doubles. Then one low D.

Sound effects

Prototypes for the slice: card stock, keys, relays, motors. Each button plays one variant.
